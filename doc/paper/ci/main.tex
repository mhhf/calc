\title{Runs Certify Their Independence:\\
  Conditional Independence on Dynamic Derivation Forests}
\author{Denis Erfurt}
\date{Draft (TODO\_0302 M7) --- assembled 2026-09-02, pending author audit}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Existing conditional-independence theory proves separation theorems
about \emph{models}: fixed Bayesian networks, proof nets, string
diagrams, type-annotated programs.  We prove one about \emph{runs}.
In \will{} --- an intuitionistic linear sequent calculus whose weighted
existential draws witnesses against declared rational priors, and whose
complete executions elaborate into kernel-checked certificates carrying
their random choices as linear \emph{draw-token} hypotheses --- every
run certificate carries a dependency graph: draw and fire events,
connected by resource flow.  We define wave-valued random variables
under \emph{random existence} (a wave may exist in only some runs,
identified across runs by ungrounded provenance), a separation
criterion on the dependency graph of a \emph{conditioning class}, and
prove soundness: separation implies that the unnormalized conditioned
mass factorizes exactly, $\mu(x,y,z) = c\cdot f(x)\cdot g(y)$, with the
factors readable as sub-forest masses and the split \emph{exhibited} by
the certificate's token partition.  Three structural discoveries shape
the criterion.  First, under unnormalized \emph{restriction} semantics
(conditioning never renormalizes), textbook d-separation is
\emph{unsound}: a wave whose total posterior weight is not constant
across the class --- through bias, or through a sort of total mass
$\neq 1$ whose draw happens in only some runs, by contingent existence,
by \emph{drop}, or by a reachable contradiction --- acts as observed
soft evidence and must enter the criterion as a conditioned virtual
child of its draw node.  Second, per-run actual-edge graphs are
unsound in principle: a run in which a context-specific rule never
fired carries no trace of it, so separation is a property of the
class, decided on a static cover and only exhibited per run.  Third,
the natural genericity converse is refuted \emph{structurally}:
deterministic fires that erase the values they consume are channels no
parameter perturbs; we prove the converse for directed value-faithful
chains by a zero-concentration witness construction and leave general
active paths conjectured.  The criterion is executable
(\texttt{certifyCI}): a decision by graph reachability over the run's
certificate and the program, verdict-tested against exact engine-computed
masses on all the counterexample programs that forced the design.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
%%====================================================================

\subsection{The problem}

Probabilistic computation is, today, trust-based at the level of its
runs.  Statically verified probabilistic programs
prove distributional facts once, about the program; probabilistic
program logics~\cite{BarthePSL2020,BaoDIBI2021,LilacPLDI2023} prove
independence judgments about program text; verifiable randomness
certifies seeds; traces emitted by probabilistic programming systems
are unchecked outputs of the very runtimes one would want to audit.
None of these produces a per-run artifact in which a probability claim
--- \emph{this} execution had \emph{this} mass, and \emph{these} parts
of it were independent --- is itself the checked object.

The companion paper~\cite{WillPaper} built the missing substrate:
\will{}, a linear sequent calculus whose weighted existential
$\erho{x}{s}A$ consumes a linear draw token $\dtok{c}{s}$, so that a
derivation's weight is a function of its \emph{endsequent} (the product
of the declared priors $\rho(c)$ over the tokens present), conserved
exactly by cut elimination; and a stochastic engine whose complete runs
elaborate into kernel-checked certificates
$\seq{\Gamma}{\Delta_0,\trz{\Tr}}{\{\exists \vec{sk}.\;\tensor\,
\mathit{residual}\}}$ whose endsequents carry their traces.  What the
certificates could not yet say is when a conditioned mass
\emph{factorizes} --- the conditional-independence question, and with
it the question of what certificates \emph{compose}.

This paper answers it.  The object is new: not a separation theorem
about a model, but about the runs of an engine whose dependency
structure is generated \emph{by} execution --- which draws exist
depends on earlier draws --- with exact rational weights, unnormalized
restriction conditioning, and a kernel-checkable artifact per run.

\subsection{Contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Wave variables under random existence}
  (\S\ref{sec:defs}).  Random variables are \emph{waves}, identified
  across runs by \emph{ungrounded provenance keys} --- computed on the
  recorded trace with existential variables in place, so that the
  draw-dependent positions are exactly the widened ones --- and defined
  on their existence events.  This is the contingent-variable reading
  of BLOG and contingent Bayesian networks~\cite{MilchCBN2005,MilchBLOG2005}
  done proof-theoretically.
\item \textbf{The class dependency graph} (\S\ref{sec:defs}).  Events
  (initial, fire, draw) connected by resource flow, where the draw
  event operationally \emph{rewrites} every fact carrying its variable
  --- so value flow is fact flow and no second edge sort exists ---
  plus latent allocation forks for linear contests and directed
  drop-decider edges.  Per-run actual-edge graphs are refuted by
  counterexample; the criterion quantifies over the conditioning class
  and is decided on a finite static cover.
\item \textbf{Mass-observed draws} (\S\ref{sec:defs},
  \S\ref{sec:criterion}).  Under restriction semantics a wave's total
  posterior weight is soft evidence; naive d-separation is unsound,
  refuted by three exact counterexamples (existence, drop,
  survival), and repaired by conditioned virtual \emph{mass-children}
  hung off draw nodes.
\item \textbf{The soundness theorem} (\S\ref{sec:soundness}):
  separation implies exact factorization of the conditioned mass, with
  the factors sub-forest masses and the split exhibited by the token
  partition.  The proof isolates the genuinely dynamic content ---
  a locality lemma, barren-subforest marginalization, and a
  recombination (exchange) argument over event configurations --- and
  cites standard moral-graph theory for the rest.
\item \textbf{Genericity, three ways} (\S\ref{sec:genericity}): the
  naive faithfulness converse is refuted structurally; a directed
  faithful-chain converse is proved by zero-concentration witnesses;
  general active paths are conjectured with a route.
\item \textbf{An executable criterion} (\S\ref{sec:impl}):
  \texttt{certifyCI} decides separation on the cover by Bayes-ball
  reachability over the certificate and the program; its verdicts are
  tested against exact engine-computed masses on every program that
  forced a design decision.
\item \textbf{The evidence-discipline substrate}
  (\S\ref{sec:evidence}--\S\ref{sec:conditioning}): single-counting
  (linearity of evidence tokens makes the posterior the correctly
  factored Bayesian product), certificate visibility (double-counting
  is invisible in the numbers, always visible in the fire
  provenances), structural splitting laws (mass factorizes over
  $\tensor$ in every derivation; no primitive weight box can say
  more), and conditioning by restriction (datasort events as checker
  premises, zero-variance importance).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Non-claims}

Completeness is not claimed and fails for three independent reasons:
parameter cancellation (as in Bayesian networks), the static cover's
conservatism, and context structure finer than the pruning predicate
--- the criterion refuses, it never asserts dependence
(context-specific separation is sound-not-complete already in the
static LDAG setting~\cite{Pensar2015}).  Statements are about the
measure, never about the pseudorandom sampler.  Modelling honesty is
out of scope by design: the theorems certify that factor structure
matches token structure; whether two tokens model independent worldly
events remains the modeller's assertion, made inspectable.

\section{Background: \will{} in brief}
\label{sec:background}
%%====================================================================

We restate the facts this paper stands on; proofs are in the companion
papers~\cite{WillPaper,TillPaper}.

\paragraph{The calculus.}  \will{} extends a graded intuitionistic
linear base with one connective and one hypothesis class:
%
\begin{mathpar}
\inferrule*[right=$\exists_\rho$-R$(c)$]
  {\seq{\Gamma}{\Delta}{A[c/x]}}
  {\seq{\Gamma}{\Delta,\,\dtok{c}{s}}{\erho{x}{s}A}}
\and
\inferrule*[right=$\exists$-L]
  {\seq{\Gamma}{\Delta,\,A[a/x]}{C}}
  {\seq{\Gamma}{\Delta,\,\erho{x}{s}A}{C}}
\and
\inferrule*[right=ghost]
  {\seq{\Gamma}{\Delta}{C}}
  {\seq{\Gamma}{\Delta,\,\dtok{c}{s}}{C}}
\end{mathpar}
%
Tokens are kernel-reserved ground linear atoms, minted only at the
draw-checker boundary; the run weight is $\prod\rho(c)$ over the
tokens in the endsequent; cut admissibility holds with exact weight
conservation~\cite{WillPaper}.

\paragraph{The engine and the measure.}  Programs are multiset rewrite
rules over linear and persistent facts; suspended existentials are
\emph{waves}; the decimation driver alternates settle-to-quiescence
segments with draws, resolving posteriors
$w(e,c) = \rho(c)\cdot\prod\{q \mid \mathsf{bias}(e,c,q,\ldots)
\text{ distinct}\}$ and, in \texttt{exact} mode, enumerating the
collapse tree.  The denoted measure $\mu$ assigns each leaf the product
of its draw weights (and \texttt{woplus} branch weights); it is
policy-independent.  \emph{Conditioning is restriction}: excluded runs
go to zero, surviving masses are untouched; renormalization exists only
in the sampler's draw probabilities.

\paragraph{Certificates.}  A sample run elaborates post-hoc-grounded
into one kernel-checked tree: \texttt{@fire} nodes re-derived from
program rule data, \texttt{@draw} nodes re-derived from the sort system
and declared priors, endsequent
$\Delta_0,\trz{\Tr} \vdash \{\exists\vec{sk}.\;\tensor\,residual\}$.
The certificate records, per fire, the consumed, produced and reserved
instances, and per draw the wave, sort/state, member and weight; the
recorded trace is \emph{ungrounded} (existential variables in place),
with the grounding substitution applied post hoc.

\section{The evidence discipline: single-counting and certificate
  visibility}
\label{sec:evidence}
%%====================================================================

The Bayesian reading of the posterior demands that conditionally
independent observations each contribute exactly one likelihood factor.
What does the \emph{syntax} guarantee?

\begin{definition}[linear-evidence discipline]
A program satisfies S1--S3 for a set $O$ of observation predicates
when: (S1) every rule antecedent mentioning an $O$-predicate is a plain
linear pattern (not \$-preserved, not promoted, not whole-bound); (S2)
no rule concludes two bias facts with the same (wave, member) pair;
(S3) bias conclusions are source-tagged per rule.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[single-counting]
\label{thm:single}
Under S1--S3, in every run and for every wave $e$ and member $c$, the
bias factors multiplied into $w(e,c)$ are in bijection with pairwise
distinct consumed observation tokens, via the fire that consumed the
observation and produced the fact:
$w(e,c) = \rho(c)\cdot\prod_{o\in O_{\mathrm{used}}(e,c)} \ell_o(c)$.
\end{theorem}

The proof is multiset accounting: S1 makes each token reach exactly one
fire, S2 bounds each fire to one factor per $(e,c)$, S3 makes the
content-addressed dedup respect fire identity.  Content-addressed set
semantics \emph{under}-identifies value-only evidence (two independent
equal-likelihood observations collapse to one factor), which is why S3
is forced: provenance is part of fact identity.

\begin{theorem}[certificate visibility]
\label{thm:visibility}
Double-counting is numerically invisible: there are program pairs ---
one honest, one sharing a \$-read observation between two bias rules
--- with \emph{identical} posteriors on the same domain.  It is always
certificate-visible: overlapping provenance requires the explicit
sharing markers (\$, promotion, whole-bind), and the certificate's
per-fire consumed/reserved records exhibit disjoint provenances for the
honest program and overlapping ones for the smuggled program, decidable
by inspection.
\end{theorem}

Independence of the evidence entering a posterior is not a property of
the posterior; it is a structural property of the derivation, and the
derivation is what ships.

\section{Splitting laws are structural}
\label{sec:splitting}
%%====================================================================

A conjectured weight-graded contraction
$\Box_{r+s}A \vdash \Box_r A \tensor \Box_s A$ (``mass splits over
independent channels'') dissolves: its three faces are already present,
in three different semirings.

\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:splitting}
(i)~Counts split additively and exactly: $!_{k+m}A \dashv\vdash
{!_k}A \tensor {!_m}A$, with both the leak and the mint refuted.
(ii)~Masses split multiplicatively and \emph{structurally}: in every
derivation ending in $\tensor$R the token zone partitions with the
context, $\Tr = \Tr_A \uplus \Tr_B$, hence
$\mathrm{mass}(D)=\mathrm{mass}(D_A)\cdot \mathrm{mass}(D_B)$ --- a
corollary of linearity, with no side condition.  (iii)~Masses sum
where derivations fork: at $\exists_\rho$ and at the weighted additive
disjunction.  Moreover any primitive box is either token-backed ---
then definable as $\trz{\Tr}\tensor A$ with derivable laws --- or a
weight-conservation leak; and the contraction shape would clone a
draw, which identity-expansion failure at $\exists_\rho$
forbids~\cite{WillPaper} and the prover refutes (one token cannot
serve two $\tensor$-channels).
\end{theorem}

Leg (ii) is the per-derivation face of everything that follows: the
$\tensor$-partition of the endsequent \emph{exhibits} the factorization
the independence theorem will produce.

\section{Conditioning by restriction}
\label{sec:conditioning}
%%====================================================================

Hard conditioning on a datasort event $S \sqsubseteq s$ (a regular tree
language declared by membership clauses) needs no new connective and no
new metatheory:

\begin{theorem}
\label{thm:restriction}
The conditioned existential $\erho{x}{S}B$ \emph{is} $\exists_\rho$
with the datasort name in its sort slot: a conditioned draw is
admissible iff the base draw is and the witness head is admitted by
$S$ --- a membership side-derivation checked at the draw oracle, never
a sequent rule.  Cut admissibility with exact weight conservation is
inherited verbatim; run mass stays $\prod\rho$ over $\trz{\Tr}$
because restriction never rescales a surviving mass.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[zero-variance importance]
\label{prop:zerovar}
On a bias-free conditioned run in which every opened wave is drawn,
the importance weight equals the inside mass $m(\mathit{root\ state})$
for every seed; in general it is $m(\mathit{root})/\prod
m(\mathit{dropped})$.  The per-draw factors telescope.
\end{proposition}

The inside masses are the sampler's normalizing constants --- solved at
load by rational Gaussian elimination, \emph{verified} inside
certificates by substitution into their own equations.  The
zero-variance identity is, in the language of the next section, the
one-wave shadow of the mass-child discipline: totals are importance
factors, and over a class they are evidence.

\section{Wave variables, the class graph, and the criterion}
\label{sec:defs}
%%====================================================================

Fix a program, initial state $\sigma_0$, and the driver's policy (the
measure is policy-independent).  Scope hypotheses: (H1) runs are
almost-surely finite under the normalized process; (H2) no settle
conflict is reachable in the class (the exact-mode discipline;
certifiable structurally); (H3) contexts are finite conjunctions of
wave-value assignments and leaf-observations of ground facts; (H4)
premises are positive, and bias rules carry no timed windows that
could delay them past their target draw.

\subsection{Events and the run graph}

A run's events are the initial event, one fire event per rule firing,
and one draw event $d_e$ per opened wave, with outcome space
$\mathrm{members}(s)\cup\{\mathsf{dropped}\}$.  The draw event is
operationally a rewrite: it consumes its suspension and every fact
instance carrying its variable and re-emits their grounded images ---
so \emph{value flow is fact flow} and d-separation's directional
discipline is literally the certificate's resource-flow discipline.
Edges: producer $\to$ consumer/reserver/reader (including persistent
premises through their recorded clause derivations), spawner $\to$
$d_e$ (existence), bias/within producer $\to$ $d_e$ (posterior reads),
$d_e \to$ consumers of grounded facts, and --- because drop-vs-draw is
decided by whoever consumes a carrier first --- a \emph{directed} edge
from every carrier consumer into $d_e$.

\subsection{Wave identity under random existence}

Certificates exist in two views: the recorded trace (existential
variables in place) and its post-hoc grounding.  \emph{Identity lives
in the ungrounded view}: provenance keys are computed recursively over
ungrounded facts, variables mapped to the keys of their waves; cohort
firings are expanded to their sequential multiset.  A \textbf{wave
variable} is a key of a draw event, defined on its existence event
$E_X$ (the runs containing an event so keyed), with value the drawn
member.  Downstream waves are genuinely contingent: a wave spawned
only in the $X{=}a$ worlds exists only there.  All statements are
division-free:

\begin{definition}[CI]
$X \CI Y \mid z$ iff for all values,
$\mu([X{=}x][Y{=}y]C_z)\cdot\mu([X{=}x'][Y{=}y']C_z)
= \mu([X{=}x][Y{=}y']C_z)\cdot\mu([X{=}x'][Y{=}y]C_z)$.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Conditioning sites}
\label{sec:sites}

Three kinds, uniformly ``nodes observed at fixed outcomes'':
Z-draws; \emph{observation colliders} $O_F$ (a virtual node with an
edge from every class-run event producing or consuming an $F$-instance
--- leaf presence is intrinsically disjunctive across the class, and
the collider modelling is what keeps the disjunction sound); and
\emph{mass-children}: for each wave $e$ let $\lambda_e(r) = T_e(r)$ if
$e$ is drawn (the total posterior weight, inside masses included) and
$1$ otherwise, non-constancy judged over the class-consistent collapse
tree \emph{including dead branches}.  If $\lambda_e$ is non-constant,
add a virtual observed node $V_e$ with edges from $d_e$ itself and
from $e$'s bias/within parents.

\begin{example}[the three leaks]
\label{ex:leaks}
Take two waves $X, Y$ over a two-member sort with priors $1, 2$.
(a)~\emph{Existence}: a wave $W$ over a sort of total prior mass $2$,
spawned exactly when $X{=}Y$, drawn and never used, gives joint masses
$2/2/2/8$: $16 \neq 4$, dependent --- though the spawn fire is an
unobserved collider textbook d-separation calls blocked.  The
normalized twin (total $1$) gives $1/2/2/4$: independent.
(b)~\emph{Drop}: a $W$ that always exists but whose carrier is
consumed before its draw exactly when $X{=}Y{=}a$ gives $1/4/4/8$:
$8 \neq 16$.  (c)~\emph{Survival}: a rule zeroing every member of $W$
exactly when $X{=}Y{=}a$ kills those runs: $0/4/4/8$, dependent
through the survival factor alone --- and on surviving leaves
$\lambda_W$ \emph{looks} constant, which is why non-constancy must see
dead branches.  All three are engine-exact test pins.
\end{example}

\subsection{The class graph and its computable cover}

The class graph $\mathcal{G}[\sigma_0 \mid z]$ is the key-identified
union of run graphs over the class, plus sites, plus latent
\emph{allocation forks} (an unobserved node into each pair of events
that can consume the same instance --- contested linear supply is an
influence channel invisible to pure fact flow).  It is a DAG on keys.

It quantifies over the class, and must:

\begin{example}[per-run graphs are unsound]
\label{ex:ctx}
A bias rule enabled only in the $X{=}a$ world makes $Y$ depend on $X$
(exact masses $3/2/2/4$; $12\neq4$), yet every $X{=}b$ certificate
contains no bias fire at all --- the connecting edge is absent from
the very runs it endangers.
\end{example}

The theorem is therefore stated for any DAG \emph{covering}
$\mathcal{G}$ (edge- and site-preserving image), and decidability comes
from a finite static cover: rule and binder nodes, edges by
template unifiability over the \emph{ungrounded} patterns (the
program's own ground constants supply argument sensitivity), context
pruning only where $z$ alone falsifies a rule.

\begin{definition}[separation]
$X \dsep Y \mid z$ iff every path between $d_X$ and $d_Y$ in the cover
is blocked given the site set $M$: a non-collider node in $M$ blocks;
a collider blocks unless it or a descendant is in $M$.
\end{definition}

\section{The soundness theorem}
\label{sec:soundness}
%%====================================================================

\begin{theorem}[soundness]
\label{thm:main}
Under H1--H4, if $X \dsep Y \mid z$ then there are $f, g$ and a
constant $c_M$ with
$\mu([X{=}x][Y{=}y]C_z) = c_M\cdot f(x)\cdot g(y)$ for all $x,y$ ---
hence $X \CI Y \mid z$.  Moreover $f$ is the conditioned mass of the
$X$-side sub-forest, $g$ of the $Y$-side, and every class certificate
exhibits the split: events, token zone
($\Tr = \Tr_A \uplus \Tr_B$), and fire provenances partition along
the criterion's sides.
\end{theorem}

Work in the ancestral closure $\An$ of $\{d_X, d_Y\}\cup M$.

\begin{lemma}[locality]
\label{lem:locality}
Class runs are exactly the maximal conflict-free causally-closed
configurations of the class's event structure satisfying $z$, one run
per (policy, seed) per configuration, with policy-independent measure.
Injectivity is by first divergent draw; surjectivity because the
derivation tree is an org chart, not a schedule; the weight product is
linearization-invariant because each draw's posterior reads only its
parents' outcomes.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[normalization decomposition]
\label{lem:decomp}
Per class run,
$\mathrm{mass}(r) = \prod_{d\in r}\hat{p}_d(\mathrm{outcome}\mid\pa)
\cdot \prod_e \lambda_e(r) \cdot \prod_{f} w_f \cdot \prod \mathbf{1}_O(r)$,
where $\hat{p}_d = w_d/T_d$ is the normalized posterior (defined on
positive-$T$ configurations), $\lambda_e$ the mass-child factors
(class-constant ones absorbed into $c_M$), $w_f$ the \texttt{woplus}
branch constants, and the indicators enforce $z$.  Every factor is a
function of one node and its parents.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[barren marginalization]
\label{lem:barren}
Nodes outside $\An$ carry $\hat{p}$-factors only (anything else would
put them in $\An$ --- in particular a wave with a reachable $T=0$ has
non-constant $\lambda$ and is not barren).  Each $\An$-projection's
fiber contributes exactly the $\An$-part's mass: zero-$T$ extensions
vanish, and over positive-$T$ extensions the normalized draws
telescope to $1$ wave by wave, using H1.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[side split; cited]
\label{lem:moral}
$X \dsep Y \mid M$ in the cover iff $M$ separates $d_X$ from $d_Y$ in
the moral graph of $\An$~\cite{Lauritzen1996,VermaPearl1988}.  Let $A$
be $d_X$'s side, $B$ the rest.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[mono-sidedness]
\label{lem:mono}
Each factor of Lemma~\ref{lem:decomp} is a function of a moral-graph
clique $\{v\}\cup\pa(v)$, hence lies wholly in $A\cup M$ or $B\cup M$.
Z-draw and $O_F$ outcomes are fixed by $z$; a $V_e$ outcome is
determined by its clique, hence by its side's projection.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[recombination]
\label{lem:exchange}
Restricted to $\An$-projections, $r \mapsto (\pi_{A\cup M}(r),
\pi_{B\cup M}(r))$ is a bijection between class projections and pairs
agreeing on the ($z$-fixed) $M$-part.  Injectivity and
well-definedness are the clique argument; surjectivity is the
exchange: the merge of two sides is causally closed, satisfies $z$,
and is conflict-free --- a cross-side contest would be an allocation
fork with edges into both sides, an active fork path contradicting
separation --- and creates no phantom enablements: an event enabled in
the merge either occurs in some class run (then its parent clique is
mono-sided and one side's maximality accounts for it) or occurs in
none (then either its premise pattern is jointly satisfiable from
both sides, putting cross-side edges on its cover node and
contradicting separation in the cover, or context pruning removed it
because $z$ falsifies it --- in every run satisfying $z$, hence in
the merge).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
By Lemmas~\ref{lem:decomp}--\ref{lem:mono} the mass of a class
$\An$-projection is an $A$-side product times a $B$-side product with
$M$-factors assigned to $A$; by Lemma~\ref{lem:exchange} the sum over
the class splits into the product of the two side sums.  The CI form
follows by direct computation; the exhibition corollary from
Theorem~\ref{thm:splitting}(ii) (tokens are linear hypotheses of their
draw events and follow their contexts) and
Theorem~\ref{thm:visibility} (side-pure fire provenances).
\end{proof}

\section{Genericity: a refutation, a converse, a conjecture}
\label{sec:genericity}
%%====================================================================

\begin{theorem}[naive faithfulness fails structurally]
\label{thm:refute}
There is a five-rule program with a fully active directed path
$d_X \to \mathit{fire} \to \mathit{bias} \to d_Y$ in whose parameter
ring $\mu(x,y) = \rho_X(x)\cdot g(y)$ \emph{identically}: two
member-discriminating rules emit the same fact $m$, which biases $Y$
equally in every world.  Deterministic fires that erase the values
they consume are channels no parameter perturbs; completeness against
the fact-flow graph is unattainable even generically.
\end{theorem}

\begin{lemma}[genericity from one witness]
\label{lem:generic}
For parameters in weight-generic positions (matching reads terms,
never weights), each mass cell is a polynomial over $\QQ_{\geq0}$
whose value at any boundary point is the true mass; a single non-zero
evaluation of the CI defect makes it a non-zero polynomial, whose
zero set generic parameters miss.
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}[faithful-chain converse]
\label{thm:chains}
If the class graph contains a directed bias-chain
$X = W_0 \to \cdots \to W_n = Y$ whose hops fire before their target
draws, discriminate two designated members of the upstream wave, and
carry dedicated free bias parameters never excluding the designated
continuations --- and the two designated paths have positive class
mass somewhere --- then for generic parameters $X \not\CI Y \mid z$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Zero-concentration: set the bias parameter of every non-designated
continuation to $0$ on each side of each hop.  By induction the chain
becomes a copy channel ($W_i = a_i$ iff $W_0 = a_0$), the cross cells
have mass $0$ and the diagonal cells positive mass, so the defect is
positive at this point; Lemma~\ref{lem:generic} concludes.
\end{proof}

Collider-activated segments, existence/drop hops, allocation-fork
hops, and infinite classes remain conjectural; the counterexample
programs of Example~\ref{ex:leaks} are single-hop witness families for
each shape.

\section{Implementation: the criterion, executable}
\label{sec:impl}
%%====================================================================

\texttt{certifyCI} decides $X \dsep Y \mid z$ from a loaded program
and one recorded run: run waves from the ungrounded trace (existential
variables are exactly the auto-widened positions); \emph{phantom}
waves for class-only existence (a spawner bounded by an init-only
trigger it already exhausted spawns nothing new; unsaturated spawn
templates get static wave nodes); static nodes for every rule, fired
or not (Example~\ref{ex:ctx}'s unfired bias rule is an edge);
premise-aligned bias targeting; allocation forks only between
mutually-unifiable consuming premises; directed drop-decider edges;
constant pruning (a node with no wave ancestor transmits nothing);
context pruning from Z-wave existence (rules starved by the
conditioned wave's spawner's single-copy trigger); and Bayes-ball
reachability~\cite{Shachter1998} with collider-descendant sites.
Refusals carry a witness walk.  Soundness is by construction --- every
approximation adds edges or sites; \texttt{separated} certifies, a
refusal never asserts dependence.

The verdicts are tested against exact engine-computed masses on all
seven programs that forced the design: the criterion certifies exactly
the four true independences (collider marginal; normalized existence
and drop twins; chain given its mediator --- the last via context
pruning) and refuses the seven dependences plus the value-erasing
chain (Theorem~\ref{thm:refute}'s program: independent, refused ---
the documented incompleteness).  Two implementation notes fed back
into the theory: the drop-decider needs a \emph{directed} edge into
the draw node (an allocation fork alone leaves the consumer a blocked
collider), and \$-read pass-throughs re-emit the identical instance
and must not count as producers.

Every mass in this paper is an executable pin
(\texttt{tests/engine/will-ci.test.js}); the evidence-discipline,
splitting-law and conditioning results are pinned in the companion
suites; complete runs ship as kernel-verified certificates
(\texttt{certifyCollapse}), with the WFC case study matching
brute-force enumeration ($41/81$ constrained maps, mass $217/625$).

%%====================================================================
\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
%%====================================================================

\paragraph{Program logics.}  PSL~\cite{BarthePSL2020} made
$\ast$/$\tensor$ probabilistic independence; DIBI~\cite{BaoDIBI2021}
gives qualitative \emph{conditional} independence as a bunched
judgment; Lilac~\cite{LilacPLDI2023} expresses CI as separation under
a disintegration modality.  All reason about program text over a fixed
sample space; none produces a per-run artifact, handles random
existence of variables, or works unnormalized.  Our
Theorem~\ref{thm:single} is the engine-level operational counterpart
of their $\ast$-reading; Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is the quantitative,
dynamic, certified transfer of DIBI's Thm~V.1.

\paragraph{Proof nets.}  The Bayesian proof-net
line~\cite{DiGuardiaEEF2026} proves disconnection $\Rightarrow$ CI on
a fixed BN-as-proof-net (their Thm~7.1) --- the sharpest static
cousin.  The net is the input; our dependency structure is generated
by execution, weights are exact and unnormalized, and the theorem's
object is the run certificate.

\paragraph{Categorical probability.}  The d-separation criterion holds
in Markov categories \emph{with conditionals} over fixed string
diagrams~\cite{FritzKlingler2023}.  \will{}'s unnormalized restriction
semantics lives where conditionals are partial
(cf.~\cite{DiLavoreRoman2023}); no separation theorem exists there,
and per-run normalized unfoldings support only a cross-check.

\paragraph{Stochastic support.}  BLOG and contingent
BNs~\cite{MilchBLOG2005,MilchCBN2005} define per-world contingent
variables but no existence-event CI calculus;
context-specific independence and
LDAGs~\cite{Boutilier1996,Pensar2015} supply the sound-not-complete
precedent and the context-pruning architecture; PLP
d-separation~\cite{RueckschlossWeitkaemper2023} works on the fixed
ground Herbrand base.  Our mass-children have no analogue in any of
these: normalized settings cannot see them.

\paragraph{Circuits and provenance.}  Decomposability --- factors with
disjoint variables --- is the d-DNNF/circuit
notion~\cite{DarwicheMarquis2002}; the conditioned trace polynomial's
indeterminate-disjoint factorization (Theorem~\ref{thm:splitting},
Theorem~\ref{thm:main}) is its proof-theoretic realization, with the
certificate exhibiting the partition
(cf.~provenance semirings~\cite{GreenKT2007}).

\paragraph{Distribution semantics.}  Sato's runs-as-derivations
conditional on a random record~\cite{Sato1995} is the operational
ancestor; the record here is a linear zone with a cut theorem and the
factorization is a theorem about it.

%%====================================================================
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%%====================================================================

The certificate a run elaborates to carries a dependency graph;
separation on it --- read at the class, with the mass-children
restriction semantics forces --- implies exact factorization of the
conditioned mass, and the certificate exhibits the factorization it
implies.  Independence claims about probabilistic executions thereby
join the run itself in the class of things a third party can check:
the graph is decidable from the artifact and the program, the factors
are sub-certificates, and every counterexample that shaped the
criterion is an executable program with exact rational masses.  What
normalized theory could not see --- that a wave's bare existence, its
drop, or its survival is evidence whenever its total weight is not
one --- is, we think, the clearest sign that the run-level object was
worth building.

Future work: the general-path genericity converse
(\S\ref{sec:genericity}); instance-refined covers (the static
quotient over-connects multi-instance rules); timed-window evidence
(the temporal sibling of order-sensitive bias); and mechanization,
shared with the companion papers' graded base.

$T{=}0$ fiber, clique-determined mass-children, the timed fence) are
repaired in the text and pinned in the test suite.
