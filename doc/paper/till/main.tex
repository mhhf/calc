\title{A Delay-Graded Lax Monad:\\
  Timed Multiset Rewriting with Proven-Exact Acceleration}
\author{Denis Erfurt}
\date{Draft (TODO\_0270)}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We present \till{}, a sequent calculus for intuitionistic linear logic
whose lax modality $\{A\}$ is \textbf{graded by a delay} from the tropical
$(\max,{+})$ dioid: availability stamps $\stmp{A}{t}$ on hypotheses combine by
$\max$ (a coeffect: synchronisation waits for the last input), delay grades
$\lax{S}{d}$ compose by $+$ (an effect: durations accumulate along a causal
chain), and a timed promotion rule stamps outputs at $\max(\text{inputs})+d$.
Grade side conditions enter the calculus as \emph{theory premises}---object-level
derivability goals over a decidable arithmetic theory---whose partial residual
$\res$ makes illegal rule instances \emph{unconstructible} rather than guarded.

We prove cut admissibility for the graded calculus---to our knowledge the first
cut-elimination result for a graded \textbf{lax/possibility} modality---and
prove identity expansion, a work/makespan separation theorem, and exact-accounting
adequacy: operational stamps are the \emph{least and only} derivable production
stamps.

The same algebra that gives the proof theory gives a scalable execution engine:
graded labelled states (THY\_0024) externalise the stamp column and stop Store
growth; forced-prefix cohort firing (THY\_0025) batches uniform populations in
constant time; orbit certificates with a covariant tie-PRF enable exact
$O(1)$ deep-time resumption.  The combined stack scales from 180\,ms/tick to
0.2\,ms/tick, eliminates Store growth over simulated weeks ($1.9\text{M} \to 161$
nodes over 4 simulated days), batches million-item populations in constant time,
and resumes 3-week idle gaps in 38\,ms with bit-exact replay.  Every optimisation
ships with a stated law and a machine-pinned differential test.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{The gap}

The Concurrent Logical Framework (CLF)~\cite{Watkins2002CLF} marks the boundary
between backward proof search and committed-choice forward chaining with a lax
modality $\{A\}$, following the judgmental reconstruction of lax
logic~\cite{FairtloughMendler1997,PfenningDavies2001}.  The modality is
ungraded: a CLF derivation of $\{A\}$ certifies reachability and nothing more.

Quantitative refinements of linear logic are standard---but on the
\textbf{necessity} side.  Bounded linear logic~\cite{GirardScedrovScott1992},
the coeffect calculi, quantitative type theory~\cite{Atkey2018},
Granule~\cite{OrchardLiepeltEades2019}, and a current line of graded sequent
calculi~\cite{MoonEadesOrchard2021,VollmerMarshallEadesOrchard2025,HanukaevEades2025}
all grade the exponential $\gbang{r} A$, and there cut elimination is settled.
Graded \emph{monads} are equally standard in
semantics~\cite{Katsumata2014,FujiiKatsumataMillies2016}---as categorical
structure, with no sequent calculus.  Granule types a graded possibility
$\Diamond_r$---as a bidirectional type system, with no proof theory.  For the lax
modality itself, the sharpest recent result is cut elimination and uniform
interpolation for \emph{ungraded} propositional lax logic~\cite{Iemhoff2024}.
\textbf{No published system gives a sequent calculus with cut elimination for a
graded lax, monadic, or possibility modality} (novelty sweeps 2026-08-18 and
2026-08-21; \S\ref{sec:related}).

Meanwhile, \emph{time} in multiset rewriting is operational folklore: timed
MSR~\cite{KanovichFORMATS2016} threads a global \texttt{Time@T} fact with a
\texttt{Tick} rule; Coloured Petri Nets~\cite{Jensen2007CPN} stamp tokens and
delay outputs with exactly the operational surface we want---and no proof theory.
Temporal session types~\cite{DasHoffmannPfenning2018} put discrete-time $\bigcirc$
on channel types for complexity analysis; intuitionistic metric temporal logic
(IMTL)~\cite{deSaToninhoPfenning2023} labels propositions with absolute intervals
and proves cut elimination---with additive label arithmetic and a constraint store,
and no resource multisets.

On the engines side, logic-based frameworks (CLF, Ceptre~\cite{Martens2015},
timed MSR) stop scaling at toy populations and short horizons: they accumulate one
proof-tree node per event, never compress time, and have no theory of
optimisation soundness.  Ad-hoc simulation engines scale, but carry no semantics:
an optimised engine can silently change reachable worlds, and replay breaks without
warning.

This paper closes both gaps: \textbf{time enters CLF-style forward chaining as a
grade}, with the proof theory done properly and the engine optimisations proven
state-identical.

\subsection{Contributions}

The contributions split into two movements.

\paragraph{(a) Proof theory.}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The calculus} (\S\ref{sec:calculus}).  ILL plus stamped atoms
  $\stmp{a}{t}$ and a delay-graded lax modality $\lax{S}{d}$, in judgmental
  presentation.  One tropical algebra plays two roles: durations compose by $+$
  in the graded bind; availability stamps combine by $\max$ in a timed promotion
  rule $\mathit{@fire}$ whose side condition is the dioid action $t \act d = t+d$.
  The calculus is parametric in an ordered-monoid/semilattice/action triple.

\item \textbf{Grade side conditions as theory premises with a partial residual}
  (\S\ref{sec:calculus}).  The residual is \emph{partial}---no proof exists when
  $F < E$---so fences like ``grades never go negative'' are not side conditions
  but \emph{Grade Preservation by construction}: no rule, present or future, can
  build an invalid grade.

\item \textbf{Metatheory} (\S\ref{sec:meta}).  Cut admissibility (three cuts:
  linear, persistent, and a graded lax cut composing $d+e$), identity expansion,
  and completeness of the counted-exponential rules.  The partial residual makes
  mismatched principal cut cases \emph{vacuous}.

\item \textbf{Max-plus activation and work/makespan separation}
  (\S\ref{sec:adequacy}).  The pure graded fragment is a \emph{cost} logic---pure
  backward derivability computes total sequential work ($\Sigma$ delays)---while
  the max-plus makespan is contributed exclusively by the stamp coeffect through
  the promotion rule.  Operational stamps are the least and only derivable
  production stamps.

\item \textbf{Processes as proof terms; atomicity by theorem} (\S\ref{sec:processes}).
  A firing with delay $d$ occupies the open interval $(a, a{+}d)$ in which no
  rule can observe its effects.  Fused rules are uninterruptible by construction;
  fission into \texttt{start}/\texttt{end} rules is their honest interruptible model.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{(b) The engine these proofs enable.}

\begin{enumerate}
\setcounter{enumi}{5}
\item \textbf{Graded labelled states} (\S\ref{sec:engine}).  Stamps live
  \emph{beside} content-addressed terms as interned label columns, not inside
  them.  Address stability follows: the Store stops growing with elapsed time
  ($1.9\text{M} \to 161$ nodes over 4 simulated days).

\item \textbf{Exact orbit acceleration} (\S\ref{sec:engine}).  Periodic orbits
  are certified as portable proofs; the phase-independence lemma makes exit-phase
  minting exact; covariant tie-PRF draws extend certification to
  multi-consumer economies with stochastic branching.  3-week idle gaps resume in
  38\,ms with bit-exact replay.

\item \textbf{Forced-prefix cohort firing} (\S\ref{sec:engine}).  A positive
  theorem (three invariants that make the batch equal the sequential $k$-prefix
  bit-exactly) whose premises are a checklist that found two live bugs.  Batched
  settle is flat in population ($\approx 6$\,ms from $10^2$ to $10^6$ items).

\item \textbf{An evaluation methodology} (\S\ref{sec:eval}).  Every optimisation
  ships with a stated law and a differential pin that asserts observational
  equivalence on the full state hash---making the pin suite a machine proof of
  the optimisation's stated law.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Non-claims}

The oracle is sound, not complete (\S\ref{sec:adequacy}); its steps are checked
structurally by the kernel and reported as unverified---trust in a bridge
derivation reduces to trust in the scheduler, by design and disclosed.  All proofs
are on paper, none machine-checked; mechanisation is future work.  The
probabilistic weight grade on the weighted additive disjunction
(\S\ref{subsec:woplus}) has operational semantics and a sequent-level law (mass
conservation), but a full probabilistic judgment ($\{A\}_w$) is a separate paper
by prior decision; it is marked as future work throughout.  The acceleration
window is voided by stamp-binding ties and \texttt{woplus} firings; the honest
limits are stated in \S\ref{sec:engine}.

\section{Grade algebras: one tropical dioid, two roles}
\label{sec:algebra}
%%====================================================================

Fix the tropical dioid $\TT = (\QQ_{\geq 0}, \max, +)$.  Its fragments:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Durations (effect)} $D = (\QQ_{\geq 0}, +, 0, \leq)$, an ordered
  commutative monoid: delays compose along causal chains.
\item \textbf{Time points (coeffect)} $T = (\QQ_{\geq 0}, \max, 0, \leq)$, a
  join-semilattice: stamps synchronise at a tensor of inputs---wait for the last.
\item \textbf{The action} ${\act} : T \times D \to T$, $t \act d = t+d$, monotone
  in both arguments, with $t \act 0 = t$, $(t \act d) \act e = t \act (d+e)$, and
  $\max(t,t') \act d = \max(t \act d,\; t' \act d)$---the semiring distributivity
  of $\TT$.
\end{itemize}

Every proof in this paper uses only these laws; the calculus is parametric in any
$(D, T, \act)$ triple (\S\ref{sec:related} discusses instances).  Stamps are
absolute points, delays relative translations---the effect/coeffect pairing is the
\emph{trivial} matched pair in the sense of~\cite{GaboardiICFP2016}; the
substantive interaction is not their distributive law $\sigma$ but the action
$\act$ inside the promotion rule.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Fenced grade algebras.}  Grades live inside a \emph{fence}
(validity predicate) $V$ with a \emph{partial residual}: $a \res b$ is the
$h \in V$ with $b + h = a$, undefined when none exists.  \till{}'s instances:

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
Grade  & Operation & Fence $V$         & Residual $\res$ \\
\midrule
delay  & $+$ & $\QQ_{\geq 0}$        & $a - b$ when $a \geq b$, else $\bot$ \\
count  & $+$ & $\NN$                  & $a - b$ when $a \geq b$ ($\NN$-closed) \\
weight & $\cdot$ & $[0,1]$           & none---$[0,1]$ closed under $\cdot$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Fenced grade algebras in \till{}.}
\label{tab:grades}
\end{table}

$V$ is closed under the exposed operations.  The residual is the graded analogue
of an Iris resource algebra's validity~\cite{JungIris2018} and a residuated
commutative monoid~\cite{Galatos2007} restricted to its valid cone.  We
deliberately do \emph{not} adopt the clamping monus ($a \mathbin{\dot{-}} b = 0$
when $b \geq a$~\cite{Amer1984}): a too-late resource must make a rule
inapplicable, not free.

\begin{theorem}[Grade Preservation]
\label{thm:grade-pres}
If every grade literal in a program satisfies its fence and rules construct grades
only through $+$ and $\res$, every grade in every reachable state---forward
execution and backward search alike---satisfies its fence.  Per-rule guards
($F \geq E$, $K \geq 1$) are derived lemmas, not stated conditions; the force of
the theorem is quantification over \emph{future} rules.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Base: literals are fence-checked at load.  Step: $a + b$ ($V$ closed); $a \res b$
(in $V$ by definition when defined; when undefined the rule did not fire).  Each
firing is a frame-preserving update for $V$.  Induction over firings; THY\_0022.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textit{In-logic reading.}  $a \res b$ is definitionally ``the $H$ with
$b + H = a$''---a derivability statement \texttt{plus H b a} over the sorted
domain.  Three faces (clause = SLD semantics; FFI = decision procedure; algebra =
$O(1)$ fast path) are fuzz-pinned to agree on definedness and value.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Design axis.}  Metric time has two proof-theoretic poles.  The
\emph{labelled/absolute} pole (IMTL~\cite{deSaToninhoPfenning2023}; timed
MSR~\cite{KanovichFORMATS2016}) uses absolute interval annotations, accumulation-only
rules, and a constraint store.  The \emph{graded/relative} pole (\till{}) has
label-free judgments and one partial operation $\res$.  Choosing grades buys
compositional, label-free judgments and pays with exactly one partial
operation---\texttt{monad\_l}'s $H := F \res E$ is the labelled system's
world-shift arithmetic $E + H = F$ folded into the grade (THY\_0022).

\section{The calculus}
\label{sec:calculus}

\subsection{Syntax and judgments}

Formulas: ILL ($\tensor, \loli, \mathbf{1}, \addconj, \bang$) extended with
\textbf{stamped atoms} $\stmp{a}{t}$ ($t \in T$; $@$ attaches to atoms only),
\textbf{counted exponentials} $\gbang{k} A$ ($k \in \NN$), and the \textbf{graded
lax monad} $\lax{S}{d}$ ($d \in D$; bare $\{S\}$ abbreviates $\lax{S}{0}$), $S$
ranging over the synchronous fragment as in CLF.

Judgments, with the lax judgment graded:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash A\tr$ --- $A$ holds of persistent context $\Gamma$
  and linear context $\Delta$.
\item $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d}$ --- $S$ is \emph{achievable} from
  $\Gamma; \Delta$ within delay bound $d$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The graded lax fragment}

\begin{mathpar}
  \inferrule[\textit{lax}]
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\tr}
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{0}}
  \and
  \inferrule[\textit{sub}]
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d} \\ d \leq d'}
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d'}}
  \and
  \inferrule[\textit{\{R\}}]
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d}}
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{S}{d}\tr}
  \and
  \inferrule[\textit{\{L\}}]
    {\Gamma; \Delta, S \vdash C\laxjdg{e}}
    {\Gamma; \Delta, \lax{S}{d} \vdash C\laxjdg{d+e}}
\end{mathpar}

\noindent\textit{lax} is the unit: what is true now is achievable with zero
delay.  \textit{\{L\}} is CLF's \emph{sticky} left rule with grade
composition---eliminating $\lax{S}{d}$ inside a lax goal adds $d$ to the bill.
\textit{sub} is subeffecting.  Erasing grades yields exactly the judgmental lax
fragment of~\cite{PfenningDavies2001}.

\medskip
\noindent Derived: graded $\mu$, $\lax{\lax{S}{d}}{e} \vdash \lax{S}{d+e}$;
functoriality; unit $S \vdash \lax{S}{0}$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Counted exponentials.}  $\gbang{k} A$ is $k$ linear parcels
($A^{\tensor k}$), with four rules (left peel/weaken, right peel/zero) whose side
conditions are arithmetic over $\NN$.

\subsection{Stamps and timed promotion}
\label{subsec:stamps}

Three laws with deliberately different standings:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Retiming} (the one sequent rule): $\Gamma; \stmp{a}{t}
  \vdash \stmp{a}{t'}\tr$ for $t \leq t'$.
\item \textbf{Ambient} $a \equiv \stmp{a}{0}$ is a canonicalisation convention at
  the state boundary, \emph{not} a rule: $a \not\vdash \stmp{a}{t}$ for every
  $t$ inside the calculus (admitting $a \vdash \stmp{a}{0}$ would compose with
  retiming into stamp forgery).
\item \textbf{Monoidal} $(A \tensor B)@t \equiv \stmp{A}{t} \tensor \stmp{B}{t}$,
  $\mathbf{1}@t \equiv \mathbf{1}$ is a meta-level law of the state representation.
\end{itemize}

The rule tying stamps to grades is the \textbf{promotion rule} in the SELL style:

\begin{mathpar}
  \inferrule[\textit{@fire}]
    {\Gamma; A_1, \ldots, A_n \vdash S\laxjdg{d}
     \\ a = \max(t_1, \ldots, t_n, 0)}
    {\Gamma;\; \stmp{A_1}{t_1}, \ldots, \stmp{A_n}{t_n}
     \;\vdash\; \stmp{S}{(a \act d)}\laxjdg{0}}
\end{mathpar}

\noindent The body promises $S$ within $d$ of its inputs; the inputs synchronise at
$a$ (coeffect $\max$); the outputs exist from $a+d$ (action); the lax grade
\emph{resets}.  Activation windows (\texttt{after} $E$ strengthens $a$;
\texttt{before} $E$ bounds it) are scheduling annotations, not connectives.

\subsection{Grade side conditions as theory premises}
\label{subsec:theory-prem}

The implemented calculus is single-level ($S\laxjdg{d}$ represented as
$\lax{S}{d}$) and its rules carry \textbf{theory premises}: object-level goals
over a decidable arithmetic theory, discharged during rule application and
re-checked by the proof kernel.  The two monad rules:

\medskip
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}r@{\;\;}l@{\quad}l@{\quad}l@{}}
$\mathit{monad\_l}$: &
  $\Gamma; \Delta, \lax{A}{E} \vdash \lax{C}{F}\tr$ &
  $\Leftarrow$ &
  $\Gamma; \Delta, A \vdash \lax{C}{H}\tr\quad
   \thprem{!\mathit{qsub}\;F\;E\;H}$ \\[4pt]
$\mathit{monad\_r}$: &
  $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{A}{E}\tr$ &
  $\Leftarrow$ &
  $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash A\tr\quad
   \thprem{!\mathit{le}\;0\;E}$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\medskip

\noindent$!\mathit{qsub}\;F\;E\;H$ derives ``$H$ is the residual $F \res E$'': it
\emph{binds} $H$ when $F \geq E$ and has \textbf{no derivation} when $F < E$.
Variables not bound by the conclusion are output variables bound by the
derivation---the exact discipline of properly oriented extra variables in
deterministic 3-CTRSs~\cite{Ohlebusch2002}, transported from rewriting to proof
theory.

Two published lineages meet here.  The premises are CLP-style constraint goals in
the sense of~\cite{LassezMcAloon1990}, living object-level in the rules.  And they
are \emph{geometric}: the residual axiom $\forall F, E.\;(F \geq E \supset \exists
H.\;F = E + H)$ is a geometric implication, so by~\cite{NegriVonPlato1998}
building it into the rules preserves cut eliminability---in our direct proof
(\S\ref{sec:meta}) this materialises as the observation that theory premises carry
no sequent-level principal formula and therefore never obstruct a permutation.

\subsection{Weighted additive disjunction}
\label{subsec:woplus}

$A \wop{q} B$ (weight $q \in [0,1] \cap \QQ$) is a \textbf{probability-graded
internal choice}: the producer decides a branch probabilistically.  Plain $\adddisj$
is unsound for the distribution semantics (it drops $q$).  The judgment is extended
with a weight annotation $\langle w\rangle$:

\begin{mathpar}
\inferrule[\textit{$\wop{q}$R$_1$}]
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d} \;\langle w\rangle}
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash (A \wop{q} B)\laxjdg{d} \;\langle q \cdot w\rangle}
\and
\inferrule[\textit{$\wop{q}$R$_2$}]
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d} \;\langle w\rangle}
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash (A \wop{q} B)\laxjdg{d} \;\langle (1-q) \cdot w\rangle}
\end{mathpar}

There is no left rule: \texttt{woplus} is consequent-only.  \textbf{Mass
Conservation}: if $\Gamma;\Delta \vdash C\laxjdg{d}$ is derivable, the derivation
forest (all proofs differing only in $\wop{q}$R choices) has weights summing to
1.  The delay and weight gradings are orthogonal.  A full probabilistic judgment
($\{A\}_w$) with its own sequent rules is deferred as a separate paper.

\begin{remark}
For the duel program \texttt{fight: rock}$\tensor$\texttt{sci -{}-o}
$\lax{\text{woplus}(3/4,\text{rock},\text{sci})}{0}$, the aggregate derivation
mass equals the combat DP $\mathrm{winProbDP}(r,s,3/4)$ exactly (rational
arithmetic, no floating-point drift).
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Presentation equivalence]
\label{thm:pres-equiv}
The judgmental two-level system and the implemented single-level system derive the
same sequents, with cut-free derivations mapping to cut-free derivations both ways,
size-linearly.
\end{theorem}

\section{Metatheory}
\label{sec:meta}
%%====================================================================

Full proofs: THY\_0023.  ``The calculus'' is the pure syntactic system (id, the
ILL rules, $\omega$-bang, counted bangs,
\textit{lax}/\textit{sub}/\textit{\{R\}}/\textit{\{L\}}, retiming, copy); the
oracle rule is excluded (\S\ref{subsec:oracle}).

\begin{theorem}[Cut admissibility]
\label{thm:cut}
The three cuts
\begin{mathpar}
  \inferrule[\textit{cut}]
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash A\tr \\ \Gamma; \Delta', A \vdash J}
    {\Gamma; \Delta, \Delta' \vdash J}
  \and
  \inferrule[$\mathit{cut}_{\mathit{lax}}$]
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d}
     \\ \Gamma; \Delta', S \vdash C\laxjdg{e}}
    {\Gamma; \Delta, \Delta' \vdash C\laxjdg{d+e}}
  \and
  \inferrule[$\mathit{cut}^{!}$]
    {\Gamma; \cdot \vdash A\tr \\ \Gamma, A; \Delta \vdash J}
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash J}
\end{mathpar}
are admissible in the cut-free calculus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Lexicographic induction on (cut-formula weight, cut kind with
$\mathit{cut}^{!} \succ \mathit{cut}_{\mathit{lax}} \succ \mathit{cut}$, the
premise derivations).  The principal $\mathit{\{R\}}/\mathit{\{L\}}$ case reduces
a true-cut on $\lax{S}{d}$ to exactly $\mathit{cut}_{\mathit{lax}}$ with grades
composing as $d+e$---no new arithmetic.  $\mathit{cut}_{\mathit{lax}}$ analyses
the left derivation: the \textit{lax} case drops to \textit{cut} with the unit
law $0+e=e$; \textit{sub} commutes by monotonicity; $\mathit{\{L\}}$ re-associates
($d' + (d_0{+}e) = (d'{+}d_0) + e$).  Retiming cuts compose by transitivity of
$\leq$.  Counted-bang principal cases use functionality of the residual ($J = J'
= K{-}1$); the mismatched pairings (peel against zero) are \textbf{vacuous}: their
theory premises demand $K \geq 1$ and $K = 0$ simultaneously, so no such pair of
derivations exists---partiality deletes case obligations.
\end{proof}

Two structural remarks:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The fence is the measure.}  Counted bangs enter the termination
  measure as $w(\gbang{K} A) = (K{+}1)(w(A){+}1)$, well-founded \emph{because}
  the count fence is $\NN$.  The delay grades---dense in $\QQ_{\geq 0}$---never
  enter the measure: every monad-cut descends to the body.
\item \textbf{No erasure lift.}  THY\_0018's original sketch routed through grade
  erasure onto the ungraded lax fragment.  The direct proof does not: the graded
  system is proved outright.
\end{itemize}

\begin{theorem}[Identity expansion]
\label{thm:id-expand}
For every formula with ground grades, $\Gamma; A \vdash A$ is derivable with
axioms only at atoms (id at unstamped atoms; retiming with reflexive $t \leq t$
at stamped atoms).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Counted-bang completeness]
\label{thm:counted-bang}
For ground $k \in \NN$, $\gbang{k} A \dashv\vdash A^{\tensor k}$ with the four
counted rules; with Theorem~\ref{thm:cut}, a sequent is provable in the counted
reading iff its expansion is provable in the bang-free fragment.  The split/merge
iso $\gbang{a+b} A \dashv\vdash \gbang{a} A \tensor \gbang{b} A$ follows.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}
\label{cor:misc}
Consistency; the analytic subformula property up to grade recomputation; and
admissibility of the compositions used by the adequacy and fusion theorems
below---they never leave the cut-free calculus.
\end{corollary}

\section{Operational semantics: timed matching and settle}
\label{sec:opsem}
%%====================================================================

A \till{} program is a set of rules $\mathit{In} \loli \lax{\mathit{Out}}{d}$; a
state is a \textbf{timed multiset}---a finite map $(\mathit{atom},
\mathit{stamp}) \to \mathit{count}$; equal-stamp copies merge into cohorts.

\subsection{Match patterns}

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Pattern          & Matches                               & Binding \\
\midrule
$A$              & any cohort of $A$                     & stamp joins activation \\
$\stmp{A}{Q}$    & any cohort                            & stamp bound to $Q$ \\
$\gbang{k} A$   & $k$ copies across any cohorts (FIFO)  & newest taken stamp activates \\
$\gbang{W} A$   & all copies                            & $W :=$ live total at firing \\
$\mathtt{read}\;A$ & as $A$, nothing consumed            & stamp joins activation \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Timed match patterns and their semantics.}
\label{tab:patterns}
\end{table}

A match $m$ assigns cohorts to pattern atoms satisfying guards.  Its
\textbf{activation} $a(m) = \max(\text{selected stamps} \cup \{\text{after-bounds}\})$,
valid iff below every \texttt{before}-bound.  Firing consumes at $a(m)$ and
produces each output stamped $a(m)+d$---one $\mathit{@fire}$ instance.

\subsection{The match order and the settle scheduler}

\begin{proposition}[Lexicographic-first is unsound]
\label{prop:lex-unsound}
Per-pattern oldest-first matching does not compute the activation-minimal match
when guards couple patterns.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Counterexample]
State $\stmp{A}{0}, \stmp{A}{3}, \stmp{B}{3}, \stmp{B}{9}$, a guard rejecting
exactly $(A@0, B@3)$: lexicographic-first finds activation 9; the minimum is 3.
Firing the 9-match ahead of another rule's 5-match violates nondecreasing
activation.  THY\_0019, Props.~1--3.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Branch-and-bound is sound and complete]
\label{prop:bab}
DFS with cohorts in ascending stamp order, carrying partial activation, pruning
branches on the partial $\max$, returns exactly the activation-minimal
FIFO-lexicographic match.  Fast path: rules without \texttt{before} windows or
cross-pattern guards admit a single-pass greedy solution---the common case costs
what untimed matching costs.
\end{proposition}

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={The settle scheduler (pseudocode).}]
settle(S, T):
  while some rule has a valid match $m$ with $a(m)$ $\leq$ T:
    among globally minimal $a(m)$ (ties: stateless PRF chooser);
    fire it (consume at $a(m)$, produce at $a(m)+d$)
  return S    -- quiescent at horizon T
\end{lstlisting}

There is no clock, no tick, no watermark: stamps are monotone, so $\settle$
resumes correctly after any horizon with no memory of it.

\begin{theorem}[Determinism and composability]
\label{thm:composability}
$\settle$ is a function of (state, horizon, seed, policies), and for
$T_1 \leq T_2$:
\[
  \settle(\settle(S,T_1),T_2) = \settle(S,T_2)
\]
---states, traces, stamps all equal.  The proof obligation is precisely that no
selection reads the horizon: minimal activation is a function of the state;
equal-activation ties are resolved by a \emph{stateless} content-derived PRF
($\mathrm{mix}(\mathit{seed} \oplus \mathrm{hash}(\mathit{state}) \oplus
\mathrm{hash}(\mathit{candidates}))$).  The law is frame-rate independence: a game
host that jumps the horizon replays exactly the history a small-stepping host
would have produced.  THY\_0019, Thms.~4--5.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Termination / Zeno guard]
\label{thm:termination}
$\settle(S,T)$ terminates iff finitely many events have activation $\leq T$;
sufficient statically: every cycle in the rule-dependency graph has positive total
delay.  $a \loli \lax{a}{0}$ from $\stmp{a}{0}$ is the Zeno counterexample; with
$\lax{a}{1}$ the same rule is productive---the delay grade as a guardedness
witness in the sense of Nakano's $\triangleright$~\cite{Nakano2000}.  Off-cycle
zero-delay rules are fine.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{settleExplore and ample sets}

\texttt{settleExplore} exhaustively expands all equal-activation conflicts,
producing a tree of worlds with edge weights from \texttt{woplus} branches.  Its
branching criterion is the \textbf{ample-set / partial-order reduction condition}
(\cite{Peled1993}): only branch on genuine conflicts (enabled matches sharing
consumed resources at the same activation).  Independent concurrent actions commute
by Theorem~\ref{thm:confluence}, so branching on them produces duplicate worlds.

\section{Adequacy}
\label{sec:adequacy}
%%====================================================================

Three results, in increasing order of what they refuse to assume.

\subsection{Exactness}
\label{subsec:exact}

\begin{theorem}[Exactness]
\label{thm:exact}
Let $R = \settle(\Delta, T)$ be the residual of an activation-ordered
conflict-free execution.  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{(Soundness)} $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{\bigotimes R}{T}$ is
  derivable---each firing is one $\mathit{@fire}$ instance, and composing the chain
  uses one $\mathit{\{L\}}$ residual per step, each \emph{defined} because
  activation order keeps the budget above the spent delay.
\item \emph{(Exactness)} For a token $\stmp{b}{u} \in R$ produced by the
  execution, the claim $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{\stmp{b}{u'}}{T'}$ is derivable
  \textbf{iff} $u' = u$: the operational stamp is the least---and \emph{only}---derivable
  production stamp.  A claimed stamp below the max-plus critical path makes some
  residual undefined; above it is blocked because the calculus omits stamped-claim
  weakening.
\item \emph{(Where weakening lives)} Hypothesis-side only for stamps ($\stmp{b}{t}
  \vdash \stmp{b}{t'}$ iff $t \leq t'$, retiming) and monad-side only for grades
  ($S \vdash \lax{S}{E}$ for $E \geq 0$, subeffecting).
\end{enumerate}
THY\_0018, Thm.~1.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Timed confluence]
\label{thm:confluence}
Conflict-free executions have order-independent residuals: the event DAG is unique,
a token's stamp is its max-plus path weight, and firing orders are linearisations.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Work adequacy and the separation theorem}

Encode a program's rules as \textbf{linear hypotheses} with multiplicities as
counted bangs: for an execution $\varepsilon$ from unstamped $\Delta_0$ with
firing multiset $n$ and residual $R$, let $\Delta_\varepsilon = \Delta_0 \uplus
\{\gbang{n(R_i)}(In_i \loli \lax{Out_i}{d_i})\}$.

\begin{theorem}[Work adequacy]
\label{thm:work}
$\cdot;\, \Delta_\varepsilon \vdash \lax{\bigotimes R}{W}$ is derivable in the
pure calculus \textbf{iff} some execution from $\Delta_0$ with firing multiset $n$
reaches residual $R$ and $W \geq \sum_i n(R_i)\cdot d_i$ (the total sequential
work).  THY\_0023, Thm.~10.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Work/makespan separation]
\label{thm:separation}
The pure grade measures work, not makespan.  Join program $\{a \loli \lax{x}{2},\;
b \loli \lax{y}{3},\; x \tensor y \loli \lax{c}{1}\}$ from $\{a, b\}$: least
pure-derivable $W$ is $6 = 2{+}3{+}1$ while $\settle$ produces $\stmp{c}{4}$
($\max(2,3){+}1$).  \textbf{Consequently: the delay-graded lax monad alone is a
cost logic---sequential composition of delays; max-plus synchronisation is
contributed exclusively by the stamp coeffect through $\mathit{@fire}$.}
THY\_0023, Thm.~11.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{The oracle rule (bridge soundness)}
\label{subsec:oracle}

The implementation realises $\mathit{@fire}$ as an admissible \textbf{oracle rule}:
for a goal $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{S}{T}$ the prover may run $\settle(\Delta,
T)$ and then prove $S$ against the residual timed multiset by the pure calculus.

\begin{theorem}[Oracle soundness]
\label{thm:oracle}
If the oracle succeeds, $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{S}{T}$ is derivable.  The
\textbf{converse is false and not claimed}: \textit{sub} derives $a \vdash
\lax{a}{d}$ for every $d \geq 0$ with no forward step.  Kernel checking of an
oracle step is structural: the settle run is trusted and \emph{disclosed}---the
checker reports it as an unverified step.
\end{theorem}

\section{Processes as proof terms: Fusion = Atomicity}
\label{sec:processes}
%%====================================================================

\begin{theorem}[Fusion = Atomicity]
\label{thm:fusion}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] \emph{Atomicity of fused rules.}  For a firing at activation $a$ with
  delay $d$, inputs are consumed at $a$ and every output carries stamp $a+d$; any
  match involving an output has activation $\geq a+d$.  The open interval
  $(a, a{+}d)$ admits no interaction: fused rules are uninterruptible by
  construction.
\item[(b)] \emph{Fission.}  Replacing $\mathit{In} \loli \lax{\mathit{Out}}{d}$
  by $\mathit{start} = \mathit{In} \loli \lax{J}{0}$, $\mathit{end} = J \loli
  \lax{\mathit{Out}}{d}$ ($J$ fresh) is observationally equivalent on $J$-free
  observables with identical stamps: $\mathit{start};\mathit{end}$ composes by
  graded $\mu$ ($0+d = d$), and fusion is cut elimination on $J$.
\item[(c)] \emph{Corollary.}  An interruptible process \textbf{must} be fissioned:
  the mid-flight resource is the honest model.  Only \emph{interaction}
  requires fission, not observability.
\end{enumerate}
THY\_0018, Thms.~3+5.
\end{theorem}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Read arcs.}  $\mathtt{read}\;A$ joins the activation max,
consumes nothing, and re-emits the token bit-identically with its original stamp.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Trace $\cong$ term.}  The flat step log (rule, $\theta$, $a$, $d$
in firing order) and the CLF monadic proof term (the let-chain) are two
presentations of one object; the stamps make the concurrency partial order
explicit.

\begin{remark}[Engineering corollary]
\label{rem:hash-accum}
Reassociating the term as an event snoc-list lets a running system prune the
settled past into a hash accumulator $H_k = \mathrm{hash}(H_{k-1}, \mathit{ev}_k)$
--- $O(1)$ history storage that cryptographically commits to the full trace.
\end{remark}

\section{From proofs to an engine}
\label{sec:engine}
%%====================================================================

The proof theory of \S\S\ref{sec:calculus}--\ref{sec:processes} determines the
engine's representation choices by theorem.  This section presents three
engineering theorems that carry proof-theoretic content:
(i)~graded labelled states (THY\_0024), (ii)~exact orbit acceleration, and
(iii)~forced-prefix cohort firing (THY\_0025).

\subsection{Graded labelled states (THY\_0024)}
\label{subsec:labelled}

\subsubsection*{The two-level split}

A graded timed calculus needs its annotations in two places:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{In the logic}: $\mathsf{at}(A, t)$ is a genuine connective (the
  $\stmp{A}{t}$ atoms, the retiming axiom, the backward prover and adequacy
  theorems reason with it).
\item \textbf{In the engine state}: the label lives \emph{outside} the term.  The
  state is rows $(A, \ell, n)$---content-addressed formula hash $A$, label $\ell$
  from the grade algebra, multiplicity $n$.  No $\mathsf{at}$-node exists at
  runtime.
\end{itemize}

This split converges from five directions: labelled
deduction~\cite{Gabbay1996,Negri2005} makes semantic parameters labels with a
separate label theory; SELL~\cite{NigamOlartePimentel2017} shows modalities are
context-zone indices; timed MSR~\cite{KanovichFORMATS2016} writes configurations
as external $(\mathit{fact}, t)$ pairs; QTT/Granule~\cite{Atkey2018,OrchardLiepeltEades2019}
put grades on context entries; differential
dataflow~\cite{MurrayMcSherry2013} uses $(\mathit{data}, \mathit{time},
\mathit{diff})$ triples.

\subsubsection*{The label algebra}

A calculus declares per label kind: \texttt{parse}/\texttt{reify}
(bijection on ground stamp terms), \texttt{cmp} (availability order),
\texttt{add} (effect monoid), \texttt{unit} (default label), and \texttt{mix}
(Zobrist contribution, value-derived).  Values are interned per-State in a
\textbf{stamp table}---session-local and compactable.

\textbf{The multiplicity column} is this same construction at the semiring $\NN$:
count is a label the engine already stores beside the address, not inside it.
Future annotation logics (provenance semirings~\cite{GreenKarvounarakisTannen2007},
spatial regions) plug in as new algebras.

\subsubsection*{Coalescing}

Arrived facts whose stamps no rule can observe are re-stamped to the unit and
merged.  The observer set---stamp-binding patterns ($\stmp{A}{Q}$,
$\gbang{k} \stmp{A}{T}$), \texttt{before}-window rules, possessed
lolis---is exactly what the \textbf{coalescer} derives as its exclusion set.  This
is the analogue of DBM LU-extrapolation in timed
automata~\cite{BengtssonYi2004}.

\begin{lemma}[Representation Adequacy]
\label{lem:rep-adequacy}
Let $\enc{\cdot}$ encode a labelled state as a formula multiset by
$\enc{(A, \ell, n)} = \mathsf{at}(A, \mathrm{reify}\;\ell)^n$ (unit labels drop
the wrapper).  Then $\enc{\cdot}$ is a bijection onto stamped multisets, and for
every rule $r$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\mathit{match}(r, S) \cong \mathit{match}(r, \enc{S})$---same assignments,
  same activations;
\item $\enc{\mathit{fire}(m, S)} = \mathit{fire}(m, \enc{S})$.
\end{enumerate}
Hence $\settle$, \texttt{settleExplore}, and every derived observable are
representation-invariant.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
A pattern observes a row through exactly two channels: the inner formula
(content address---identical) and the label, which enters only through binding
sites and guard computation, all passing through \texttt{parse}/\texttt{reify}
(mutually inverse on ground stamp terms).
\end{proof}

\subsubsection*{Address stability (operational payoff)}

With labels inside the term: every $(\mathit{inner} \times \mathit{stamp})$
combination interns an immortal arena entry, every firing pays a hash-cons insert,
and shifting the time origin re-interns the live state.  Measured on the PP2 shell
+ kiln: $\approx 0.4$M dead nodes per simulated day, $\approx 15\%$ of settle CPU
in interning.

With labels beside the term: addresses are time-stable.  \textbf{Rebase} is a
table rebuild-swap of the live rows into a fresh stamp table---$O(\mathit{live})$,
zero term allocation, compaction built in.

\textit{Measured}: 4 simulated days grow the Store by 161 nodes (was $\approx
1.9$M), $\approx 12{,}000\times$ reduction; live stamp table 4--5 entries after
each rebase; CPU neutral.

\subsection{Exact orbit acceleration}
\label{subsec:acceleration}

\subsubsection*{Periodic orbit certificates}

A $\settle$ that certifies a periodic orbit mints a portable proof:

\begin{lstlisting}[language={}]
{ "v": 1, "fingerprint": ..., "sigKey": ..., "period": [n, d],
  "sinkDelta": [...], "cycleEvents": n }
\end{lstlisting}

($\approx 458$ bytes in practice.)  A later $\settle(\mathit{state}, T,
\{\mathit{certificate}\})$ revalidates at the current frontier and applies the
elapsed cycles as ONE jump.

\subsubsection*{Phase-Independence Lemma}

The in-run proof certifies recurrence at checkpoint phase $t_0$:
$\mathit{state}(t_0 + p) = \mathit{shift}_p(\mathit{state}(t_0))$ with no
draw-sensitive tie in the window.  Determinism outside such ties propagates:
$\mathit{state}(t + p) = \mathit{shift}_p(\mathit{state}(t))$ for every $t \geq
t_0$.  A stale certificate silently falls back to in-run re-detection.

\textit{Measured (A1)}: 3-week idle resume = 1 jump, 23.9M events elided, 38\,ms,
bit-exact vs.\ from-scratch replay; certificate 458 bytes.

\subsubsection*{Covariant tie-PRF draws (A3a)}

The tie chooser's PRF input is \textbf{translation-covariant} for ties whose rules
bind no stamp position: seed $+$ the tied candidates' canonical frontier-relative
identities (rule, consumed cohorts at frontier-relative stamps, stamp-free
$\theta$)---nothing absolute.

\begin{remark}[Seeded-PRF chooser determinism]
\label{rem:prf-det}
This covariance is load-bearing for ``state-identical including every draw'': an
exact orbit recurrence \emph{forces the draw to replay}, so tie-poisoned cycles
certify exactly.  The covariant key encodes coalesce-eligible cohorts at their
normal form and aggregates takes per (inner, stamp), so coalesce cadence and cohort
split/merge cannot leak into draws---$\texttt{accelerate} \equiv \texttt{coalesce}$
holds even on drawing programs.
\end{remark}

\textit{Measured (A3a)}: kiln (multi-consumer economy with PRF tie draws) 3-week
resume = 1 certificate jump, 23.8M events elided, 10.6\,ms, bit-exact (was
491\,s of chunked unrolling; OOM before that).

\subsubsection*{What voids the acceleration window}

The window is voided by: \textbf{stamp-binding ties} (a \texttt{before}-window
rule or $\stmp{A}{Q}$ pattern makes the draw identity depend on absolute time,
breaking covariance); \textbf{\texttt{woplus} firings} (each fire draws its own
branch; the draw stream is not translation-covariant---acceleration must re-prove
one period live, A3b).  Ground deadlines (\texttt{before 47}) are handled
honestly: the jump is capped strictly below the deadline.

\subsection{Forced-prefix cohort firing (THY\_0025)}
\label{subsec:cohort-firing}

\subsubsection*{The wrong theorem}

The natural justification for firing a match once at multiplicity
$k$---``pairwise confluence, any interleaving reaches the batched state''---proves
the wrong thing.  \textbf{Confluence is a statement about fires the scheduler
performs; it says nothing about whether it performs them.}

\textit{Counterexample}: \texttt{spoil}: $\texttt{wood} \loli \lax{\mathbf{1}}{0}$ and
\texttt{saw}: $\texttt{wood} \loli \lax{\texttt{plank}}{2}$ tied at one instant
over a $10^6$-wood cohort.  Batching \texttt{spoil} consumes the whole cohort; the
sequential run interleaves \texttt{spoil} and \texttt{saw} by PRF draws.  The
batched state is a world no sequential execution reaches---a soundness bug, not an
optimisation.

\begin{theorem}[Forced-Prefix Batching]
\label{thm:forced-prefix}
Let $m$ be the unique candidate at instant $a$ (no tie), with per-fire consumption
$C$ and reservation $R$.  Suppose firing $m$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] produces no linear output landing at $a$ (every delay strictly
  positive, or zero-delay outputs feed no rule/loli antecedent, no wildcard in
  scope);
\item[(ii)] produces no persistent fact;
\item[(iii)] draws nothing per fire (no \texttt{woplus}, no existential
  resolution);
\item[(iv)] is not a possessed loli.
\end{enumerate}
Then for every $k \leq \min_{\text{consumed rows}} \lfloor(\mathit{count} -
R)/C\rfloor$, the batched step equals the sequential $k$-prefix---state-identical
including the Zobrist hash, the pending schedule, and every subsequent PRF draw.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof (induction on $k-1$ intermediate states)]
After each fire: (i) removals only raise other rules' minimal activations; no
produced fact creates a new candidate at $a$; (ii) the persistent zone is
untouched; (iii) the deterministic matcher (FIFO over stable rows) recomputes the
SAME match, still unique.  State-identity is then exact because FactSet mutations
hash final counts and no randomness is consumed inside the batch.
\end{proof}

\subsubsection*{A theorem's premises are a checklist for the implementation}

Working the proof conditions found \textbf{two live bugs}:

\paragraph{Bug 1 (condition (ii)---persistent production).} The implementation's
instant-feeding test returned ``not feeding'' for any positive delay before
examining persistent consequents.  A persistent fact produced under a delayed monad
is still visible at the instant (the persistent zone carries no labels), so it can
enable a competitor no delay test sees.  Fix: check persistent consequents first.

\paragraph{Bug 2 (condition (i)---possessed lolis).} ``Feeds a rule antecedent''
was keyed off the static rule list, but possessed lolis are candidate sources too.
A zero-delay output only a state loli consumes slipped the guard.  Fix: extend the
antecedent tables with state-loli antecedents at each check site.

\subsubsection*{Multiplicity and serialisation}

$k = \min_{\text{consumed rows}} \lfloor(\mathit{count} - \mathit{reserved})/C\rfloor$
subsumes structural guards: $\gbang{W} A$ binds the whole pool $\to$ floor $= 1$
(one cohort); preserved machines $(\$\mathtt{saw})$ serialise the next round by
reproduced copies landing at $a \act d$.  \textbf{Serialisation is emergent} from
resource counting.

\textit{Measured (B1)}: batched settle is flat in population---$\approx 6$\,ms
from $10^2$ to $10^6$ items---while per-item scales linearly (715\,ms at $10^5$);
$\times 114$ at $10^5$, $\approx \times k$ beyond.

\section{Implementation and evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}
%%====================================================================

\subsection{The artifact}

\till{} is implemented in \calc{}, a proof-calculus sandbox with a
content-addressed formula store (formulas are hashes; $O(1)$ equality).
Components: the backward focused prover over the declarative rule file (template
rules with theory premises; the arithmetic theory is itself a logic program over
exact rationals, with an FFI decision-procedure face as an optimisation---\emph{theory
is semantics, FFI is optimisation}: every FFI predicate has clause definitions, and
a no-FFI mode runs the entire test surface on the clause face alone); the
independent proof-checking kernel (re-derives every step including theory premises,
re-threads linear resource accounting, rejects context-leaking forgeries; oracle
and eigenvariable steps disclosed in an \texttt{unverified} report); the timed
scheduler (branch-and-bound minimal-activation matching, stateless PRF chooser);
and a TTY shell for live timed programs.

\subsection{Executable metatheory}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Provability grid}: $\sim$90 pinned provable/refuted sequents from
  \S\ref{sec:meta}, every proof kernel-verified with zero unverified steps.
\item \textbf{Adequacy suites}: executable specifications whose \texttt{\#expect}
  gates are wrapped as sequents (Theorem~\ref{thm:exact} soundness + refutations;
  Theorem~\ref{thm:fusion} as (under)derivability); the pure work-adequacy suite
  (Theorems~\ref{thm:work}/\ref{thm:separation}, no oracle in any proof path,
  oracle column cross-checked against a from-the-spec reference).
\item \textbf{Differential fuzzing}: (1) grade arithmetic three ways---FFI $\|$
  clause resolution $\|$ BigInt reference---including residual definedness; (2)
  scheduler vs the executable reference semantics; (3) random monad towers
  $\lax{\cdots\lax{a}{d_1}\cdots}{d_n} \vdash \lax{a}{f}$ against the $\sum d$
  oracle, plus retiming and production-claim exactness on random one-rule programs.
  The exactness clause of Theorem~\ref{thm:exact} was \emph{corrected by this
  harness} (an earlier sketch wrongly admitted production-stamp weakening; the
  fuzzer found the counterexample).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Methodology: differential pinning}

Every optimisation is a state morphism with a stated law.  The pin suite asserts
the law mechanically:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{State-identical}: batched settle (B1), labelled states
  (THY\_0024 rebase), and acceleration (A1) are each pinned against per-item /
  unoptimised / unrolled paths on the same seed.  Observational equivalence is
  asserted on the full state hash (Zobrist), not just selected outputs.
\item \textbf{World-preserving} (weaker): coalescing and rebase are asserted to
  preserve the reachable-worlds semantics.  Their pins assert that
  \texttt{settleExplore} tree structure is identical before and after.
\item \textbf{Bound-preserving} (weakest): the certificate-resume path pins event
  totals and schedule shape.
\end{itemize}

The pin suite has $\approx 3{,}250$ tests including 14 cohort-firing arms,
differential correctness arms (FFI $\|$ clause $\|$ BigInt reference for grade
arithmetic), and ILL-native provability tests.  No measurement below is reported
as an asymptotic claim unsupported by a differential pin.

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
%%====================================================================

\paragraph{Lax modality, sequent rules, cut elimination.}
\cite{FairtloughMendler1997}; \cite{PfenningDavies2001}; CLF~\cite{Watkins2002CLF};
\cite{Iemhoff2024} (cut elim + uniform interpolation, ungraded).
Ungraded throughout: we add the grade and keep the shape;
Theorem~\ref{thm:cut} is the graded extension of Iemhoff's 2024 result.

\paragraph{Graded monads (semantics).}
\cite{Katsumata2014}; \cite{FujiiKatsumataMillies2016}.
Categorical; no sequent calculus.

\paragraph{Graded necessity, sequent proof theory.}
BLL~\cite{GirardScedrovScott1992};
\cite{MoonEadesOrchard2021};
\cite{VollmerMarshallEadesOrchard2025} (mixed linear/graded sequent calculus,
graded comonad);
\cite{HanukaevEades2025};
GRASS~\cite{GRASS2026}.
All grade $\gbang{r}$; no graded lax/possibility sequent calculus anywhere.

\paragraph{Graded possibility.}
Granule's $\Diamond_r$~\cite{OrchardLiepeltEades2019}: type system only; the
proof theory of $\lax{S}{d}$ is precisely the missing piece.

\paragraph{Effect--coeffect interaction.}
\cite{GaboardiICFP2016}: our pair is their trivial case; the timed content is the
$\act$ action inside promotion.

\paragraph{Timestamps in MSR.}
\cite{KanovichFORMATS2016} (+ resilience line~\cite{KanovichResilient2024}):
global clock fact + Tick; no max-plus activation, no in-flight representation;
their progressing condition $\approx$ our Zeno guard (globally where ours is
cycle-local).

\paragraph{Metric time, labelled sequents.}
IMTL~\cite{deSaToninhoPfenning2023}: the labelled/absolute pole (interval labels
$A^{[a,b]}$, constraint store, additive label arithmetic, a split rule for
temporal uncertainty, no operational semantics).  \till{} is the graded/relative
pole: label-free judgments, one partial $\res$.  The axis is \emph{residuation
vs.\ accumulation}---residuation is the price of internalising time as grades
(THY\_0022).  CLF has no grades; timed MSR has no backward calculus; IMTL has no
resources.  The adequacy bridge (THY\_0018 Theorem~1) fills the gap.

\paragraph{Timed tokens, arc delays.}
CPN Tools~\cite{Jensen2007CPN,JensenKristensenWells2007}; timed-arc Petri nets;
timed automata~\cite{AlurDill1994}.
Identical operational surface, no proof theory.

\paragraph{Time in linear type systems.}
\cite{DasHoffmannPfenning2018}: discrete $\bigcirc$ on channel types for
complexity; no graded lax modality over multisets, no $\QQ$ delays, no tropical
activation.

\paragraph{$({\max},{+})$ systems theory.}
\cite{Baccelli1992}: the algebra and eigenvalue theory, imported; not connected to
logic.

\paragraph{In-flight $=$ proof-term node.}
Event structures~\cite{NielsenPlotkinWinskel1981}: the let-node identification
and atomicity-as-theorem are ours.

\paragraph{Constraint side conditions.}
\cite{LassezMcAloon1990}; \cite{NegriVonPlato1998};
\cite{Ohlebusch2002} (3-CTRS); Twelf constraint domains; deduction modulo.
See \S\ref{subsec:theory-prem}---object-level decidable theory premises with
functional output binding in dynamic assumptions; unconditional cut elimination.

\paragraph{Timed automata acceleration.}
Zone abstraction~\cite{BengtssonYi2004} and flat
acceleration~\cite{BoigelotFAST}: \till{}'s orbit certificates are analogous but
semantically derived from the calculus.  DBM LU-extrapolation $\approx$ our
coalescing.

\paragraph{Petri nets, GAMMA, P systems.}
Step semantics~\cite{BestDevillers1987} and GAMMA~\cite{BanatreLeMetayer1990}
fire multisets preserving reachability but not the identity of a scheduled run.
P systems~\cite{Paun2000} fire under maximal parallelism.  Forced-prefix batching
(\S\ref{subsec:cohort-firing}) is the interleaving-semantics counterpart---a
$k$-uniform step inside committed choice, exact per seed.

\paragraph{Partial-order reduction.}
\cite{Peled1993}: \texttt{settleExplore} uses the same criterion; forced-prefix
batching inverts it: POR asks when reorderings \emph{may} be collapsed; the
forced prefix asks when the scheduler \emph{must} repeat.

\paragraph{Differential dataflow / provenance semirings.}
\cite{MurrayMcSherry2013} $(\mathit{data}, \mathit{time}, \mathit{diff})$ triples
and consolidation are the systems sibling of our labelled state construction;
the coalescer corresponds to consolidation.
\cite{GreenKarvounarakisTannen2007}: the label algebra is exactly a provenance
semiring; the stamp algebra $(\QQ_{\geq 0}, \max, +)$ is one instance.

\paragraph{Linear logic programming and CLF.}
Ceptre~\cite{Martens2015} is the nearest applied neighbour: a linear logic language
for interactive simulation with no graded modalities, no time, and no proof that
optimisations preserve semantics.

\medskip
\noindent Novelty was stress-tested adversarially (three sweeps, 30+ papers, last
on 2026-08-21 covering CSL/LICS/POPL/FSCD/ESOP 2024--26): no published system
gives a sequent calculus with cut elimination for a graded lax, monadic, or
possibility modality; no system embeds max-plus activation in a promotion rule; no
graded modal system has operationally partial grade subtraction.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%%====================================================================

Grading CLF's lax monad by a tropical delay yields a sequent calculus where
scheduling \emph{is} proof search structure: durations compose in the graded bind,
synchronisation is a promotion side condition, in-flight work is a proof-term node,
and the scheduler is a sound, deterministic, frame-rate-independent oracle.  The
metatheory is done in full---cut admissibility, identity expansion, exact
adequacy, and a separation theorem showing the grade alone measures cost while
stamps alone contribute scheduling.

The same algebra drives the engine: graded labelled states give address stability
($1.9\text{M} \to 161$ nodes / 4 days), forced-prefix cohort firing gives
population-flat batching ($\times 114$ at $10^5$), and covariant orbit
certificates give exact deep-time resumption (38\,ms / 3 weeks, bit-exact).  Every
optimisation ships with a proof and a machine pin.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Future work.}  Mechanisation of \S\ref{sec:meta} (the rules must
carry the theory-premise form).  The probabilistic weight grade---a full
$\{A\}_w$ judgment with a second cut ($+$ for delays, $\cdot$ for
weights)---is a separate paper.  Stochastic aggregate jumps via the Kalman-closure
criterion (A3b) would reduce a week of stochastic combat to one matrix power.
Partitioned clocks (multiple independent time dimensions) and whole-program
specialisation with translation validation are further directions.
