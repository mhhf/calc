\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{till}---timed intuitionistic linear logic---a graded sequent
calculus whose execution semantics IS the logic: multiset rewriting is forward
proof search, and every state transition is a derivable sequent.  till extends
intuitionistic linear logic with a delay-graded lax monad $\laxm{A}{d}$ (the
critical-path delay as a tropical $(\max,{+})$ grade), graded bangs $!_k A$ and
$!_W A$ (counted and whole-cohort exponentials), a probability-weighted internal
choice $A \wop{q} B$, and fenced grade algebras that make grade invalidity mean
rule inapplicability, not a side-condition obligation.  The execution
model---the \texttt{settle} scheduler---is a deterministic, horizon-split-invariant
function of the state.  Every performance optimization ships with a proven
state-identity law and a machine-pinned differential test: the index is
optimization, the multiset is semantics.  The combined stack scales a
symbolic-execution engine from 180\,ms/tick to 0.2\,ms/tick, eliminates Store
growth over simulated weeks ($1.9\text{M} \to 161$ nodes over 4 simulated days),
batches million-item populations in constant time, and resumes 3-week idle gaps
in 38\,ms with bit-exact replay.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{The problem}

Symbolic execution over linear resources---concurrent protocol verification, game
mechanics, economic simulations, contract replay---inhabits a no-man's land
between two unsatisfying extremes.  On one side, logic-based frameworks (CLF,
Ceptre, timed multiset rewriting) provide clean semantics but stop scaling at toy
populations and short time horizons: they accumulate one proof tree node per
event, never compress time, and have no theory of optimization soundness.  On the
other, ad-hoc game or simulation engines scale, but carry no semantics: an
optimized engine can silently change reachable worlds, performance bugs become
logic bugs, and replay breaks without warning.

The specific failure modes are concrete: a timed symbolic engine accumulates a
hash-cons arena entry for every (atom, timestamp) combination it mints---an
immortal cost that grows with elapsed simulation time, not with live state size.
A naive scheduler scans the full population for each rule activation; a
million-item cohort costs a million steps.  Accelerating past an idle period
requires unrolling every intervening event.  None of these costs are inherent to
the problem---they are artifacts of conflating the semantics-carrying
representation with the operational one.

\subsection{The doctrine}

till is organized around one design axiom: \textbf{an optimization may never
change the world}.  Formally, every optimization is a morphism on states with a
stated law (state-identity, world-preservation, or bound-preservation), and every
such law is mechanically pinned by a differential test that runs both paths and
asserts observational equivalence.  A claimed optimization that cannot be pinned
is not shipped.  A pin failure is a soundness bug, not a performance regression.

The corollary: \textbf{loud refusal over silent wrongness}.  When a guard's
premise fails---a grade residual is undefined, a cohort count is zero, a
covariance check fails---the rule simply does not fire.  No clamping, no
wraparound, no silent default.  The partial grade residual (\S\ref{sec:fenced-grades})
is the algebraic expression of this principle: undefinedness is inapplicability,
not an error to be caught.

\subsection{Contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The delay-graded lax monad $\laxm{A}{d}$} (\S\ref{sec:calculus}):
  the first sequent calculus for a graded lax/possibility modality, with cut
  admissibility and identity expansion proved in full (discharging all of CLF's
  prior sketches for the ungraded case extended to grades).  The tropical
  $(\max,{+})$ algebra as both the activation coeffect and the delay effect, with
  a single cut whose two-line proof needs only the ordered-commutative-monoid laws.

\item \textbf{Fenced grade algebras} (\S\ref{sec:fenced-grades}): a partial
  residual whose undefinedness equals rule inapplicability, removing all per-rule
  side-condition obligations by making illegal grades unconstructible.  Grade
  Preservation holds by construction for all rules, including future ones.

\item \textbf{The work/makespan separation theorem}
  (\S\ref{sec:metatheory}): pure backward derivability measures total sequential
  work ($\Sigma$ delays); the bridge (\texttt{@fire} promotion rule $+$
  \texttt{settle}) measures makespan (max-plus critical path).  The stamp layer
  is not a conservative decoration of the graded monad---it is a provable
  extension.

\item \textbf{Graded labelled states} (\S\ref{sec:labelled-states}): stamps live
  beside content-addressed terms as interned label columns, not inside them.
  Address stability follows: the Store stops growing with elapsed time.  The count
  column (multiplicity) and the stamp column are the same construction over
  different grade algebras ($\N$ vs $\Qnn$).

\item \textbf{Exact orbit acceleration} (\S\ref{sec:acceleration}): periodic
  orbits are certified as portable proofs (mint\,/\,validate\,/\,resume); the
  phase-independence lemma makes exit-phase minting exact; covariant tie-PRF draws
  extend certification to multi-consumer economies with stochastic branching.

\item \textbf{Forced-prefix cohort firing} (\S\ref{sec:cohort-firing}): a
  negative result (confluence proves the wrong thing for a draw-based
  committed-choice scheduler), a positive theorem (three invariants that make the
  scheduler repeat itself exactly $k$ times, so the batch equals the sequential
  $k$-prefix bit-exactly), and the observation that a theorem's premises are a
  checklist for the implementation---two live bugs found by working the proof
  conditions.

\item \textbf{An evaluation methodology} (\S\ref{sec:evaluation}): differential
  pinning as a first-class contribution, not just a testing practice---every
  optimization is pinned state-identical against its unoptimized path, making the
  pin suite a machine proof of the optimization's stated law.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Background: ILL, CLF, and the CALC engine}
\label{sec:background}

till is implemented inside CALC, a proof calculus platform for intuitionistic
linear logic (ILL) with a content-addressed term store.  ILL's resource
interpretation---every hypothesis consumed exactly once---makes it a natural
semantics for state: a resource held is a proposition true, a resource consumed
is a fact used.  CLF~\cite{WatkinsCervesatoPfenningWalker02} extends ILL with a
lax monad $\laxb{A}$ marking the boundary between backward proof search and
forward multiset rewriting.  CALC implements this five-layer architecture: a
content-addressed kernel, generic search primitives, Andreoli focusing, strategy
layers (manual and auto backward; committed-choice and exhaustive forward), and a
LNL/ILL layer.

ILL connectives: tensor $\tensor$, linear implication $\loli$, unit $\mathbf{1}$,
additive conjunction $\with$, additive disjunction $\adddisj$, exponential $!$,
existential $\exists$, universal $\forall$.  The lax monad $\laxb{A}$ (CLF's
boundary marker) becomes $\laxm{A}{d}$ in till, graded by a delay from the
tropical dioid.

\section{The till Calculus}
\label{sec:calculus}

\subsection{The grade algebra}
\label{sec:grade-algebra}

till's graded modalities are organized around the tropical dioid
$\Ttrop = (\Qnn, \max, +)$:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Delays (effect)} $D = (\Qnn, +, 0, \leq)$---delays compose by $+$
  along a production chain (critical path).
\item \textbf{Time points (coeffect)} $T = (\Qnn, \max, 0, \leq)$---availability
  stamps combine by $\max$ at a tensor of inputs (synchronize: wait for the last
  input).
\item \textbf{The action} $\action : T \times D \to T$, $t \action d = t + d$---a
  duration translates a time point.  Laws: $t \action 0 = t$,
  $(t \action d) \action e = t \action (d + e)$, and
  $\max(t,t') \action d = \max(t \action d, t' \action d)$ (the tropical semiring
  law).
\end{itemize}

Every proof and theorem in \S\S\ref{sec:calculus}--\ref{sec:cohort-firing} uses
only these laws.  The calculus is therefore parametric: any (ordered commutative
monoid $D$, join-semilattice $T$, monotone distributive action $\action$)
instantiates it.  The tropical instance is the timed one.

\subsection{Syntax and rules}
\label{sec:syntax-rules}

Formulas extend ILL with stamped atoms $A@t$ (availability at time $t \in T$)
and the graded monad $\laxm{S}{d}$ (delay $d \in D$; bare $\laxb{S}$ abbreviates
$\laxm{S}{0}$):

\paragraph{Judgments} (following the judgmental lax logic of
Pfenning--Davies~\cite{PfenningDavies01}):

\begin{itemize}
\item $\Gamma;\Delta \vdash A\;\mathsf{true}$---$A$ holds of persistent $\Gamma$
  and linear $\Delta$.
\item $\Gamma;\Delta \vdash S\;\mathsf{lax}@d$---$S$ is achievable within delay
  $d$ of the context's availability (delay is relative; stamps make it absolute).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{The graded fragment} (ILL rules unchanged):

\begin{mathpar}
\inferrule[right=lax]
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jtrue{S} }
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jlax{S}{0} }
\and
\inferrule[right=sub]
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jlax{S}{d} \\ d \leq d' }
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jlax{S}{d'} }
\and
\inferrule[right={$\{\}R$}]
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jlax{S}{d} }
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jtrue{\laxm{S}{d}} }
\and
\inferrule[right={$\{\}L$}]
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jlax{S}{d} \\
    \Gamma;\Delta',S \vdash \jlax{C}{e} }
  { \Gamma;\Delta,\laxm{S}{d} \vdash \jlax{C}{d+e} }
\end{mathpar}

$\{\}L$ is CLF's sticky left rule with grade composition: eliminating $\laxm{S}{d}$
inside a lax goal adds $d$ to the grade.  Derived: $\laxm{\laxm{S}{d}}{e} \vdash
\laxm{S}{d+e}$ (graded $\mu$---delays along a bind chain add),
$S \vdash \laxm{S}{0}$ (unit $\eta$).

\paragraph{The timed promotion rule} ties stamps to grades:

\begin{mathpar}
\inferrule[right=@fire]
  { \Gamma;\,A_1,\ldots,A_n \vdash \jlax{S}{d} \\
    a = \max(t_1,\ldots,t_n,0) }
  { \Gamma;\,A_1@t_1,\ldots,A_n@t_n \;\vdash\; S@(a \action d)\;\mathsf{lax}@0 }
\end{mathpar}

where $S@u$ stamps every atom of $S$ with $u$.  Inputs synchronize at $a$
(coeffect $\max$); outputs exist from $a + d$ (action $\action$); the lax grade
resets to 0.  This is the SELL promotion rule with a global side condition.

\paragraph{Counted bangs}: $!_k A$ ($k$ copies at any ages) and $!_W A$ (whole
cohort, $W$ the live count at firing time).  Peel rules (\texttt{!Rpeel}/\texttt{!Lpeel})
with theory premises \texttt{<- !qsub K 1 J} (the partial residual---\S\ref{sec:fenced-grades}).

\paragraph{Activation windows}: \texttt{after E} (strengthens the activation max)
and \texttt{before E} (validity deadline) are scheduling annotations on
\texttt{@fire}, not connectives.  They have no proof-theoretic footprint beyond
the side condition.

\paragraph{Weighted additive disjunction} $A \wop{q} B$ (\S\ref{sec:woplus}):
$q \in [0,1] \cap \Q$, producer-side probabilistic choice.

\paragraph{Refinement sorts} (\S\ref{sec:refinement-sorts}, brief): extrinsic
Curry-style refinements over content-addressed terms.  Subsort edges are
persistent facts; the loader materializes the reflexive-transitive closure once,
making in-logic subsort queries total fact lookups.

\subsection{Fenced grade algebras}
\label{sec:fenced-grades}

Each grade in till lives in a \textbf{fenced grade algebra}
$(G, \oplus, 0, \leq, V, \res)$:

\begin{itemize}
\item $V \subseteq G$ is the \textbf{fence} (validity predicate): $0 \in V$, $V$
  closed under $\oplus$.
\item $\res$ is the \textbf{partial residual}: $a \res b$ is the $h \in V$ with
  $b \oplus h = a$, \textbf{undefined} when no such $h$ exists.
\end{itemize}

till's instances:

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
grade  & $\oplus$ & fence $V$         & $\res$ \\
\midrule
delay  & $+$ & $v \geq 0$            & $a - b$ when $a \geq b$, else $\bot$ \\
count  & $+$ & $v \in \N$            & $a - b$ when $a \geq b$ ($\N$-closed) \\
weight & $\cdot$ & $0 \leq v \leq 1$ & none---$[0,1]$ is closed under $\cdot$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Fenced grade algebras in till.}
\label{tab:grades}
\end{table}

\begin{theorem}[Grade Preservation]
\label{thm:grade-pres}
If every grade literal satisfies its fence, and every rule constructs grades only
through $\oplus$ and $\res$, then every grade in every reachable state---forward
and backward---satisfies its fence.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Base: literals are fence-checked at load.  Step: $a \oplus b$ ($V$ closed);
$a \res b$ (in $V$ by definition when defined; when undefined the rule did not
fire).  Each firing is a frame-preserving update for $V$.  Crucially, the
quantification is over \emph{all future rules}---no per-rule side condition is
needed.
\end{proof}

The force: per-rule guards (\texttt{F >= E}, \texttt{K >= 1}) are derived lemmas,
now deleted from till's rules file.  The partial residual's undefinedness IS the
guard.

\paragraph{The in-logic reading.} $a \res b$ is definitionally ``the $H$ with
$b + H = a$''---a derivability statement \texttt{plus H b a} over the sorted
domain.  Over bin naturals no clause constructs a negative $H$, so the fence is
\emph{derivational}.  Three faces (clause $=$ SLD semantics; FFI $=$ decision
procedure; algebra $=$ $O(1)$ fast path) are fuzz-pinned to agree on definedness
and value.

\paragraph{Design axis.} Metric time has two proof-theoretic poles.  The
labeled/absolute pole (IMTL, de~S{\'a}--Toninho--Pfenning
PPDP'23~\cite{deSaToninhoPfenning23}; timed MSR~\cite{KanovichKiriginNigamScedrovTalcott16})
uses absolute interval annotations $A^{[a,b]}$, accumulation-only rules, a
constraint store $\Omega$, and a proof-branching \emph{split} rule when $\Omega$
cannot order two times.  The graded/relative pole (till) has label-free judgments
and one partial operation $\res$.  Choosing grades buys compositional, label-free
judgments and pays with exactly one partial operation---\texttt{monad\_l}'s
$H := F \res E$ is the labeled system's world-shift arithmetic $E + H = F$ folded
into the grade.

\subsection{Metatheory (THY\_0023)}
\label{sec:metatheory}

\begin{theorem}[Cut Admissibility]
\label{thm:cut}
Three cuts are admissible in the cut-free system: linear $\mathsf{cut}$, lax
$\mathsf{cut\_lax}$ (with grade composition $d + e$), and persistent
$\mathsf{cut}!$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{mathpar}
\inferrule[right=cut\_lax]
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jlax{S}{d} \\
    \Gamma;\Delta',S \vdash \jlax{C}{e} }
  { \Gamma;\Delta,\Delta' \vdash \jlax{C}{d+e} }
\end{mathpar}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Lexicographic induction on (formula weight, cut kind, derivation height).  The
principal $\laxm{S}{d}$ case ($\{\}R$ vs $\{\}L$) reduces to $\mathsf{cut\_lax}$
on body $S$---the composition $d + e$ is exactly what $\{\}L$ already performs,
so no new grade arithmetic appears.  Crucially, the mismatched counted-bang cases
($!R_{\mathrm{peel}}$ vs $!L_0$, $!R_0$ vs $!L_{\mathrm{peel}}$) are
\textbf{vacuous} by theory-premise partiality: \texttt{!qsub K 1 J} is derivable
only when $K \geq 1$, so a case demanding simultaneously $K = 0$ has no witness.
The partial residual deletes principal cut cases rather than adding proof
obligations.  Commutative cases permute the cut past grade-passive rules using
only monotonicity and associativity of $+$.  Grade Preservation
(Theorem~\ref{thm:grade-pres}) ensures the measure is well-founded: the count
fence $V = \N$ is exactly what makes the induction terminate for counted bangs;
the density of $\Q$ delays is harmless because delay grades never enter the
measure.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Identity Expansion]
For every formula $A$ with ground grades, $\Gamma;\,A \vdash A$ is derivable
using axioms only at atoms.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Counted-Bang Completeness]
$!_k A \dashv\vdash A \tensor \cdots \tensor A$ ($k$ copies) by the
counted-bang peel/zero rules only; the four rules are complete for the
tensor-power semantics.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Work Adequacy]
For ground $W \geq 0$: $\cdot;\,\Delta_\varepsilon \vdash \laxm{\tensor R}{W}$
is derivable in the pure calculus iff there is an execution from $\Delta_0$ with
firing multiset $n$ and residual $R$ such that $W \geq \sum_i n(R_i) \cdot d_i$
(the total sequential work).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Work/Makespan Separation]
The pure graded monad fragment measures total sequential work ($\Sigma$ delays);
\texttt{settle} $+$ stamps measure makespan (max-plus critical path).  For the
join program $\{a \loli \laxm{x}{2},\; b \loli \laxm{y}{3},\; x \tensor y \loli
\laxm{c}{1}\}$ from $\{a, b\}$:
\begin{itemize}
\item least $W$ with pure derivability: $6 = 2 + 3 + 1$
\item \texttt{settle} output: $c@4$ ($\max(2,3) + 1$).
\end{itemize}
The stamp layer is a genuine extension of the graded-monad fragment, not a
conservative decoration.  The bridge-soundness theorem (\S\ref{sec:execution}) is
precisely the statement that this extension is consistent with the pure fragment.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Weighted additive disjunction (\texttt{woplus})}
\label{sec:woplus}

$A \wop{q} B$ (weight $q \in [0,1] \cap \Q$) is a
\textbf{probability-graded internal choice}: the producer decides a branch
probabilistically.  A plain $\adddisj$ reading is unsound for the distribution
semantics (it drops $q$).  The judgment is extended with a weight annotation
$\langle w\rangle$:

\begin{mathpar}
\inferrule[right={$+[q]R_1$}]
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jlaxw{A}{d}{w} }
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jlaxw{A \wop{q} B}{d}{q \cdot w} }
\and
\inferrule[right={$+[q]R_2$}]
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jlaxw{B}{d}{w} }
  { \Gamma;\Delta \vdash \jlaxw{A \wop{q} B}{d}{(1-q) \cdot w} }
\end{mathpar}

There is no left rule: \texttt{woplus} is consequent-only.  \textbf{Mass
Conservation}: if $\Gamma;\Delta \vdash C\;\mathsf{lax}@d$ is derivable, the
derivation forest (all proofs differing only in $+[q]R_1/R_2$ choices) has
weights summing to 1.  \textbf{Derivation Forest $\cong$ Absorbing Markov
Chain}: for the duel program
\texttt{fight: rock * sci -{}-o \{woplus 3/4 rock sci\}}, the aggregate
derivation mass equals the combat DP $\mathrm{winProbDP}(r,s,3/4)$ exactly
(rational arithmetic, no floating-point drift).  The delay and weight gradings
are orthogonal: the combined cut has delays adding and weights multiplying,
realized in one cut rule.

\subsection{Refinement sorts (brief)}
\label{sec:refinement-sorts}

Content addressing forces extrinsic (Curry-style) sorts: with sort baked into
the hash, $5{:}\mathit{bin} \neq 5{:}q$, breaking subsumption and requiring
coercions.  Instead, sort membership is a provable persistent judgment; the
loader materializes the reflexive-transitive closure of declared subsort edges as
ground facts once at load, making in-logic subsort queries total lookups.  The
FFI principle one level up: the sort table is optimization, membership proof is
semantics.

\section{Execution: Timed Matching and the Settle Scheduler}
\label{sec:execution}

\subsection{States and matches}
\label{sec:states-matches}

A \textbf{timed multiset state} is a finite map from (atom, stamp) pairs to
positive counts; an (atom, stamp, count) triple is a \textbf{cohort}.  Rule
antecedents have timed patterns:

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
pattern        & matches                              & binds \\
\midrule
\texttt{A}     & any cohort of $A$                    & stamp joins activation \\
\texttt{A@Q}   & any cohort                           & stamp to $Q$ \\
\texttt{!\_k A}& $k$ copies across any cohorts (FIFO) & newest taken stamp activates \\
\texttt{!\_W A}& all copies                           & $W :=$ live total \\
\texttt{read A}& as $A$, nothing consumed             & stamp joins activation \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Timed match patterns and their semantics.}
\label{tab:patterns}
\end{table}

A match $m$ is an injective assignment of cohorts to patterns satisfying guards.
Its \textbf{activation} $a(m) = \max(\text{selected stamps} \cup \{E : \texttt{after}\ E\})$.
Firing consumes inputs at $a(m)$ and produces each output $B$ under $\laxb{\cdot}@d$
as $B@(a(m)+d)$.

\subsection{The match order: minimal activation, FIFO tie-break}
\label{sec:match-order}

\begin{proposition}[Lexicographic-first is unsound]
Enumerating cohorts oldest-first and taking the first valid assignment does not
compute the minimal-activation match when guards or windows couple patterns.
\textit{Counterexample}: state $A@0, A@3, B@3, B@9$; guard rejecting
$(A@0, B@3)$.  Lexicographic-first finds $(A@0, B@9)$ with $a = 9$; minimal is
$(A@3, B@3)$ with $a = 3$.  A scheduler firing $a = 9$ ahead of another rule's
$a = 5$ match violates nondecreasing activation.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Branch-and-bound is sound and complete]
DFS with cohorts in ascending stamp order, carrying partial activation $a_0$,
pruning branches with $a_0 \geq \mathit{best\text{-}so\text{-}far}$, returns
exactly the activation-minimal FIFO-lexicographic match.  \textit{Fast path}:
rules without \texttt{before} windows or cross-pattern guards admit a single-pass
greedy solution.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{The settle scheduler (D12)}
\label{sec:settle}

\begin{lstlisting}
settle(S, T):
  while some rule has a valid match $m$ with $a(m) \leq T$:
    among matches with globally minimal $a(m)$: if several, chooser;
    fire it; update $S$
  return $S$    -- quiescent at $T$; future stamps pending
\end{lstlisting}

There is \textbf{no clock, no tick, no watermark}: stamps are monotone, so after
$\mathtt{settle}(T)$ every remaining match has activation $> T$ and a later call
resumes correctly with no memory of $T$.

\paragraph{Chooser (P5).} Equal-activation conflicts are resolved by a
\textbf{stateless content-derived PRF}:
$\mathit{choice} = \mathrm{mix}(\mathit{seed} \oplus \mathrm{hash}(\mathit{state}) \oplus
\mathrm{hash}(\mathit{candidate\ set}))$.
No RNG state exists in engine or state; determinism is a function of (seed,
state, candidate set).

\begin{theorem}[Composability / Frame-Rate Independence, E5]
\label{thm:composability}
For $T_1 \leq T_2$:
$\mathtt{settle}(\mathtt{settle}(S, T_1), T_2) = \mathtt{settle}(S, T_2)$---states,
traces, and stamps all equal.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
While the next event's activation is $\leq T_1$, both sides select the same
match: minimal activation is a function of state only, and the PRF reads (seed,
state, candidate set)---none of which mentions the horizon.  When the next
activation exceeds $T_1$ the inner settle returns without firing; the outer
continues the same sequence.  The horizon gates the loop but never influences a
selection---that is the entire proof obligation, discharged by construction.
\end{proof}

This is what makes a game host frame-rate independent and offline catch-up exact.

\begin{theorem}[Determinism]
\texttt{settle} is a function of (state, horizon, seed, policies).
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Termination]
Sufficient static condition: every cycle in the rule-dependency graph has
positive total delay.  \textit{Zeno counterexample}: $a \loli \laxm{a}{0}$
fires forever at one instant.  The delay grade is a guardedness witness
(Nakano's~\cite{Nakano00} $\bullet$ modality): every recursion through
$\laxb{\cdot}@d$ for $d > 0$ advances the schedule.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{settleExplore and ample sets}
\label{sec:settle-explore}

\texttt{settleExplore} exhaustively expands all equal-activation conflicts,
producing a tree of worlds with edge weights from \texttt{woplus} branches.  Its
branching criterion is the \textbf{ample-set / partial-order reduction condition}:
only branch on genuine conflicts (enabled matches that share consumed resources at
the same activation).  Independent concurrent actions commute---their tensor
derivation is order-invariant by Theorem (Timed Confluence)---so branching on
them produces duplicate worlds.

\section{Graded Labelled States (THY\_0024)}
\label{sec:labelled-states}

\subsection{The two-level split}
\label{sec:two-level}

A graded timed calculus needs its annotations in two places:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{In the logic}: $\mathsf{at} : \mathit{formula} \to \mathit{delay}
  \to \mathit{formula}$ is a genuine connective (the $A@t$ atoms, the retiming
  axiom $\mathtt{at\_l}: A@T_1 \vdash A@T_2 \leftarrow\ !{\le}\ T_1\ T_2$, the
  backward prover and adequacy theorems reason with it).  This is hybrid logic's
  satisfaction operator, internalized.
\item \textbf{In the engine state}: the label lives OUTSIDE the term.  The state
  is rows $(A, \ell, n)$---content-addressed formula hash $A$, label $\ell$ from
  the grade algebra, multiplicity $n$.  No $\mathsf{at}$-node exists at runtime.
\end{itemize}

The literature converges on this split from five directions: labelled
deduction~\cite{Gabbay96,Negri05} makes semantic parameters labels with a
separate label theory; SELL~\cite{NigamOlartePimentel17} shows modalities are
context-zone indices; timed MSR~\cite{KanovichKiriginNigamScedrovTalcott16}
writes configurations as external $(\mathit{fact}, t)$ pairs;
QTT/Granule~\cite{Atkey18,OrchardLiepeltEades19} put grades on context entries
$x :^\rho A$; differential dataflow~\cite{MurrayMcsherry13} uses
$(\mathit{data}, \mathit{time}, \mathit{diff})$ triples.  The only precedent for
time INSIDE interned fact identity is temporal Datalog's $\mathsf{holds}(P, T)$---the
state-explosion pattern zone abstraction was invented to escape.

\subsection{The label algebra}
\label{sec:label-algebra}

A calculus declares per label kind:

\begin{lstlisting}
parse  : term -> value         -- ground stamp term -> algebra value
reify  : value -> term         -- lazy internalization (memoized)
cmp    : value x value -> ord  -- availability order (scheduling)
add    : value x value -> value -- effect monoid (delay composition)
unit   : value                -- default label
mix    : value -> int32        -- Zobrist contribution (value-derived)
\end{lstlisting}

Values are interned per-State in a \textbf{stamp table} (value $\leftrightarrow$
small id, with cached float, mix, and reified term).  The table is session-local
and compactable---unlike the append-only Store arena, dead labels die.

\textbf{The multiplicity column} of run-length states is this same construction
at the semiring $\N$: count is a label the engine already stores beside the
address, not inside it.  Future annotation logics (provenance semirings, spatial
regions, epistemic indices) plug in as new algebras, not new engine code.

\subsection{Adequacy}
\label{sec:adequacy}

\begin{lemma}[Representation Adequacy]
\label{lem:rep-adequacy}
Let $\enc{\cdot}$ encode a labelled state as a formula multiset by
$\enc{(A, \ell, n)} = \mathsf{at}(A, \mathrm{reify}\ \ell)^n$ (unit labels drop
the wrapper).  Then $\enc{\cdot}$ is a bijection onto stamped multisets, and for
every rule $r$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathit{match}(r, S) \cong \mathit{match}(r, \enc{S})$---same
  assignments, same activations
\item $\enc{\mathit{fire}(m, S)} = \mathit{fire}(m, \enc{S})$
\end{itemize}
Hence \texttt{settle}, \texttt{settleExplore}, and every derived observable are
representation-invariant.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
A pattern observes a row through exactly two channels: the inner formula (content
address---identical) and the label, which enters only through binding sites and
guard computation, all passing through $\mathrm{parse}/\mathrm{reify}$ (mutually
inverse on ground stamp terms).  Output labels are produced only by the firing
law, computed identically in both representations.
\end{proof}

Two observables are contractually excluded: the 32-bit state hash and the
equal-label enumeration tiebreak.  Both are representation-internal names;
programs depending on them are exactly the draw-sensitive ones, covered by
\texttt{settleExplore}'s any-world contract.

\subsection{The observer boundary}
\label{sec:observer-boundary}

A label needs term-level existence only where the logic can see it.  The observer
set---stamp-binding patterns (\texttt{A@Q}, \texttt{!\_k A@T}),
\texttt{before}-window rules, possessed lolis---is precisely what the
\textbf{coalescer} already derives as its exclusion set.  Reification is therefore
lazy and rare: a program observing most stamps would neither coalesce nor certify,
and already sits outside the fast fragment.  The millions of unobserved timestamps
a long run mints never touch the Store.

\textbf{Coalescing}: arrived facts whose stamps no rule can observe are
re-stamped to the unit and merged.  This is the analog of DBM LU-extrapolation
in timed automata~\cite{BengtssonYi04}.

\subsection{Address stability (the operational payoff)}
\label{sec:address-stability}

With labels inside the term ($\mathsf{at}(\mathit{inner}, \mathit{stamp})$ as a
Store node): every $(\mathit{inner} \times \mathit{stamp})$ combination interns an
immortal arena entry, every firing pays a hash-cons insert, and shifting the time
origin re-interns the live state.  Measured on PP2 shell $+$ kiln: $\approx
0.4$M dead nodes per simulated day, $\approx 15\%$ of settle CPU in interning.

With labels beside the term: addresses are time-stable.  The Store stops growing
with elapsed time.  \textbf{Rebase} is a table rebuild-swap of the live rows into
a fresh stamp table---$O(\mathit{live})$, zero term allocation, compaction built
in.  (A uniform in-place shift was rejected: a shifted value can collide with the
unit label, breaking value injectivity that Zobrist hashing and row dedup
require.)

\textit{Measured}: 4 simulated days grow the Store by 161 nodes (was $\approx
1.9$M), $\approx 12{,}000\times$ reduction; live stamp table 4--5 entries after
each rebase; CPU neutral.

\section{Exact Acceleration}
\label{sec:acceleration}

\subsection{Periodic orbit certificates}
\label{sec:orbit-certs}

A \texttt{settle} that certifies a periodic orbit mints a portable proof:

\begin{lstlisting}[language={}]
{ "v": 1, "fingerprint": ..., "sigKey": ..., "period": [n, d],
  "sinkDelta": [...], "cycleEvents": n }
\end{lstlisting}

(JSON-safe, $\approx 458$ bytes in practice.)  A later
$\mathtt{settle}(\mathit{state}, T, \{\mathit{certificate}\})$ revalidates it at
the current frontier---same probe as an in-run checkpoint---and applies the
elapsed cycles as ONE jump.

\paragraph{Phase-Independence Lemma.} The in-run proof certifies recurrence at
checkpoint phase $t_0$: $\mathit{state}(t_0 + p) = \mathit{shift}_p(\mathit{state}(t_0))$
with no draw-sensitive tie in the window.  Firing is deterministic outside such
ties, and tie draw-sensitivity is translation- and sink-invariant, so determinism
propagates: $\mathit{state}(t + p) = \mathit{shift}_p(\mathit{state}(t))$ for
every $t \geq t_0$.  The exit phase therefore inherits the proven period,
per-period sink growth, and per-period event count.

A stale or tampered certificate silently falls back to in-run re-detection: a
stale certificate can never mis-fire.

\textit{Measured (A1)}: 3-week idle resume $=$ 1 jump, 23.9M events elided,
38\,ms, bit-exact vs.\ from-scratch replay; certificate 458 bytes.

\subsection{What voids a window (honest negative space)}
\label{sec:void-window}

The acceleration window is voided by:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Stamp-binding ties}: a \texttt{before}-window rule or stamp-binding
  pattern (\texttt{A@Q}) makes the draw identity depend on absolute time
  $\to$ absolute input $\to$ not covariant $\to$ orbit period may differ across
  phases.
\item \textbf{\texttt{woplus} firings}: each fire draws its own branch; the draw
  stream is not translation-covariant $\to$ acceleration must re-prove one period
  live (A3b).
\end{itemize}

Ground deadlines (\texttt{before 47}) are handled honestly: the jump is capped
strictly below the deadline; normal firing crosses it; the post-deadline regime
re-certifies.

\subsection{Covariant tie-PRF draws (A3a)}
\label{sec:covariant-prf}

The tie chooser's PRF input is \textbf{translation-covariant} for ties whose
rules bind no stamp position: seed $+$ the tied candidates' canonical
frontier-relative identities (rule, consumed cohorts at frontier-relative stamps,
stamp-free $\theta$)---nothing absolute.

Consequence: an exact orbit recurrence \textbf{forces the draw to replay}.
Tie-poisoned cycles (any multi-consumer economy where $!_W\;\mathtt{wood}$ races
the sawmill) certify exactly---the draws no longer void the acceleration window.

The covariant key encodes coalesce-eligible cohorts at their normal form and
aggregates takes per (inner, stamp), so coalesce cadence and cohort split/merge
cannot leak into draws---$\mathtt{accelerate} \equiv \mathtt{coalesce}$ holds
even on drawing programs.

\textit{Measured (A3a)}: kiln (multi-consumer economy with PRF tie draws) 3-week
resume $=$ 1 certificate jump, 23.8M events elided, 10.6\,ms, bit-exact (was
491\,s of chunked unrolling; OOM before that).

\section{Forced-Prefix Cohort Firing (THY\_0025)}
\label{sec:cohort-firing}

\subsection{The wrong theorem}
\label{sec:wrong-theorem}

The natural justification for firing a match once at multiplicity $k$---``the
$k$ single fires use disjoint resources under one substitution, so they are
pairwise confluent, and any interleaving reaches the batched state''---proves the
wrong thing.

\textbf{Confluence is a statement about fires the scheduler PERFORMS; it says
nothing about WHETHER the scheduler performs them.}

\textit{Counterexample}: \texttt{spoil: wood -{}-o \{I\}} and
\texttt{saw: wood -{}-o \{plank\}@2} tied at one instant over a $10^6$-wood
cohort.  Batching \texttt{spoil} consumes the whole cohort; the sequential run
interleaves \texttt{spoil} and \texttt{saw} by PRF draws.  The batched state is
not merely a reordering---it is a world \textbf{no sequential execution reaches}.
Under the exact-replay invariant (optimization may never change the world), that
is a soundness bug, not an optimization.

Petri-net step semantics~\cite{BestDevillers87}, GAMMA~\cite{BanatreLeMet90},
and P systems~\cite{Paun00} all fire multisets in one step, but their parallel
step is an any-world device---it preserves reachability, not the identity of a
scheduled run.

\subsection{The forced-prefix theorem}
\label{sec:forced-prefix}

\begin{theorem}[Forced-Prefix Batching]
\label{thm:forced-prefix}
Let $m$ be the unique candidate at instant $a$ (no tie), with per-fire
consumption $C$ and reservation $R$.  Suppose firing $m$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item produces no linear output landing at $a$ (every delay strictly positive, or
  zero-delay outputs feed no rule/loli antecedent, no wildcard in scope);
\item produces no persistent fact;
\item draws nothing per fire (no \texttt{woplus}, no existential resolution);
\item is not a possessed loli.
\end{enumerate}
Then for every $k \leq \min_{\text{consumed rows}} \lfloor(\mathit{count} -
R)/C\rfloor$, the batched step equals the sequential $k$-prefix---state-identical
including the Zobrist hash, the pending schedule, and every subsequent PRF draw.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof (induction on the $k{-}1$ intermediate states)]
After each fire: (i) removals only raise other rules' minimal activations
(activation is a min over a shrinking candidate set); no produced fact creates a
new candidate at $a$; (ii) the persistent zone---timeless, hence instantly visible
at any delay---is untouched; the resource bound guarantees the same rows still
cover $C + R$, so the deterministic matcher (FIFO enumeration over stable rows)
recomputes the SAME match, still unique.  State-identity is then exact because
FactSet mutations hash final counts and the draw stream consumed no randomness
inside the batch.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A theorem's premises are a checklist for the implementation}
\label{sec:checklist}

Working the proof conditions found \textbf{two live bugs}:

\paragraph{Bug 1 (condition (ii)---persistent production).} The implementation's
instant-feeding test returned ``not feeding'' for any positive delay BEFORE
examining persistent consequents.  A persistent fact produced under a DELAYED
monad is still visible at the instant (the persistent zone carries no
labels---\S\ref{sec:two-level}), so it can enable a competitor no delay test sees.
A delayed $!k$ producer tied with a consumer of the cohort a $!k$-guarded rule
also wanted: the $d$-world vanished in \texttt{settleExplore}.  Fix: check
persistent consequents first.  Pin: \texttt{till-settle}'s
persistent-arcs containment arm.

\paragraph{Bug 2 (condition (i)---possessed lolis).} The implementation keyed
``feeds a rule antecedent'' off the static rule list, but possessed lolis are
candidate sources too.  A zero-delay output only a state loli consumes slipped the
guard: a batch ran past the loli competitor, and the grow/loli tie draw vanished
from the PRF stream.  Fix: extend the antecedent tables with state-loli
antecedents at each check site.  Pin: \texttt{till-batch}'s loli fence arm.

This pattern---premises as an implementation checklist---is a general methodology:
state the theorem, work the premises, audit the code against each premise.

\subsection{The multiplicity witness}
\label{sec:multiplicity-witness}

$k = \min_{\text{consumed rows}} \lfloor(\mathit{count} -
\mathit{reserved})/C\rfloor$ is not merely a bound---it subsumes the structural
guards one would otherwise state:
\begin{itemize}
\item $!_W A$ binds and takes the whole pool $\to$ floor $= 1$ (first cohort
  only)
\item age-agnostic spread take exhausts every cohort but its last $\to$ floor $= 1$
\item preserved machine (\texttt{\$saw}, consumed and re-produced) has count $=$
  \#machines $\to$ $k$ same-stamp machines batch $k$-parallel; reproduced copies
  land at $a \otimes d$ and serialize the next round
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Serialization is emergent} from resource counting---no machine-specific
rule is needed.  Reads subtract because each fire re-reads the pool.

\subsection{Zeno preservation}
\label{sec:zeno}

A zero-progress loop must feed its own instant---its output IS its
antecedent---so condition~(i) excludes it from batching.  The instant guard
therefore keeps its meaning under batching (\texttt{maxInstantSteps} now counts
rule progress rather than tokens), and no divergent program can batch its way past
it.

\textit{Measured (B1)}: batched settle is \textbf{FLAT in population}---$\approx
6\,\text{ms}$ from $10^2$ to $10^6$ items---while per-item scales linearly
(715\,ms at $10^5$); $\times 114$ at $10^5$, $\approx \times k$ beyond.

\subsection{Methodology: differential pinning}
\label{sec:methodology}

Every optimization is a state morphism with a stated law.  The pin suite asserts
the law mechanically:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{State-identical}: batched settle (B1), labelled states (THY\_0024
  rebase), and acceleration (A1) are each pinned against per-item / unoptimized /
  unrolled paths on the same seed.  Observational equivalence is asserted on the
  full state hash (Zobrist), not just selected outputs.
\item \textbf{World-preserving} (weaker): coalescing and rebase are asserted to
  preserve the reachable-worlds semantics, not a specific world.  Their pins
  assert that \texttt{settleExplore} tree structure is identical before and after.
\item \textbf{Bound-preserving} (weakest): the certificate-resume path pins event
  totals and schedule shape, not individual draw outcomes (which may change if the
  resumed state is coalesced differently).
\end{itemize}

The pin suite has $\approx 3{,}250$ tests including 14 cohort-firing arms
(motivating case, differentials, serialized machines, read arcs, spread takes,
all fence guards, E5 chunking), differential correctness arms (FFI $\|$ clause $\|$

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\paragraph{Linear logic programming and CLF.} till is built on CLF's lax
monad~\cite{WatkinsCervesatoPfenningWalker02}---the design is explicitly a
conservative graded decoration of CLF's shape.
Ceptre~\cite{Martens15} is the nearest applied neighbor: a linear logic language
for interactive simulation.  Ceptre has no graded modalities, no time, no proof
that optimizations preserve semantics.

\paragraph{Timed multiset rewriting.}
Kanovich--Kirigin--Nigam--Scedrov--Talcott (FORMATS
2016)~\cite{KanovichKiriginNigamScedrovTalcott16} add time to MSR via a global
\texttt{Time@T} fact and a \texttt{Tick} rule.  This has no proof-theoretic reading
of durations, no in-flight job representation, no max-plus activation semantics,
and no backward calculus for the timed fragment.  Their ``progressing'' condition
approximates our Zeno guard (our version is the weaker cycle-local form: only
cycles need positive total delay).

\paragraph{Graded modalities.}
BLL~\cite{GirardScedrovScott92} grades the exponential $!^n$;
QTT~\cite{Atkey18}, coeffect calculi~\cite{GaboardiKatsumataOrchardBreuvartUustalu16},
and Granule~\cite{OrchardLiepeltEades19} grade contexts and types by resource
semirings.  Hanukaev--Eades (CSL 2025)~\cite{HanukaevEades25} gives the nearest
graded sequent calculus with cut elimination---but grades the necessity side
($!_r$), never a lax/possibility modality.  Granule types a graded $\Diamond_r$
but as a bidirectional type system, with no sequent calculus and no
cut-elimination theorem.  The delay-graded lax modality $\laxm{A}{d}$ with
sequent rules and cut admissibility has no prior.

\paragraph{Intuitionistic Metric Temporal Logic.}
de~S{\'a}--Toninho--Pfenning (PPDP 2023)~\cite{deSaToninhoPfenning23} gives a
labeled absolute system $A^{[a,b]}$ with a constraint store and a
proof-branching split rule.  This is the labeled/absolute pole of
\S\ref{sec:fenced-grades}'s design axis; till is the graded/relative pole.  IMTL
has no operational semantics; timed MSR has no backward calculus; CLF has no
grades.  The adequacy bridge (THY\_0018 Theorem~1) fills the gap.

\paragraph{Timed automata acceleration.}
Zone abstraction~\cite{BengtssonYi04} and flat
acceleration~\cite{BoigelotFAST} compress regular timed runs.  till's orbit
certificates are analogous but semantically derived from the calculus: the
phase-independence lemma is a theorem about the graded monad, not a zone widening
heuristic.  DBM LU-extrapolation $\approx$ our coalescing (label-column rewrite to
unit).

\paragraph{Petri nets, GAMMA, P systems.}
Step semantics~\cite{BestDevillers87} and GAMMA~\cite{BanatreLeMet90} fire
multisets of transitions in one step, preserving reachability but not the identity
of a scheduled run.  P systems~\cite{Paun00} fire under maximal parallelism
(any-world).  Forced-prefix batching (\S\ref{sec:cohort-firing}) is the
interleaving-semantics counterpart---a $k$-uniform step inside committed choice,
exact per seed.

\paragraph{Partial-order reduction.}
Peled (CAV 1993)~\cite{Peled93} and the ample-set condition give a criterion for
collapsing concurrent independent actions.  \texttt{settleExplore} uses the same
criterion (only branch on genuine conflicts---matches sharing consumed resources at
the same activation).  Forced-prefix batching inverts the POR condition: POR asks
when reorderings MAY be collapsed; the forced prefix asks when the scheduler MUST
repeat---the collapse is not a choice among worlds but the world.

\paragraph{Differential dataflow.}
McSherry et~al.'s~\cite{MurrayMcsherry13} $(\mathit{data}, \mathit{time},
\mathit{diff})$ triples and consolidation are the systems sibling of our labelled
state construction (\S\ref{sec:labelled-states}); the coalescer's function
corresponds to consolidation.  Differential dataflow targets incremental query
evaluation over arbitrary lattices; till targets logic programs with a
committed-choice operational semantics.

\paragraph{Provenance semirings.}
Green--Karvounarakis--Tannen (PODS 2007)~\cite{GreenKarvounarakisTannen07}:
annotation column over a commutative semiring.  The label algebra in
\S\ref{sec:label-algebra} is exactly this construction; the stamp algebra
$(\Qnn, \max, +)$ is one instance.

\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

till makes execution semantics and logic the same object: every forward step is a
provable sequent, every optimization ships with a law and a pin.  The stack
achieves: $900\times$ tick improvement (180\,ms $\to$ 0.2\,ms), $\approx
12{,}000\times$ Store compression (4 days), $\times 114$ to $\approx \times k$
population batching, and week-scale idle replay in $<40\,\text{ms}$ with
bit-exact results.

\paragraph{Future work.}

\textbf{(A3b) Stochastic aggregate jumps with the Kalman-closure criterion.}
\texttt{woplus} firings currently void the acceleration window (each fire draws
independently).  A Markov chain whose transition matrix is \emph{affine} in the
current distribution can be jumped by matrix exponentiation; the criterion is
whether the chain's per-step update closes over a finite basis under convex
combination (a Kalman-style reachability condition, checking the span of the
Jacobian of the update map at the orbit point).  For the duel program this would
reduce a week of stochastic combat to one matrix power.

\textbf{(Partitioned clocks.)} The current stamp algebra is a single $\Qnn$
join-semilattice.  Partitioned clocks (multiple independent time dimensions,
e.g.\ physical time and logical turn counter) would allow concurrent rules on
disjoint clocks to compose without spurious synchronization at $\max$.  The label
algebra in \S\ref{sec:label-algebra} is parametric in the carrier; a product
algebra with per-dimension scheduling is the minimal extension.

\textbf{(Whole-program specialization with translation validation.)} The
translation-covariance checker (\S\ref{sec:covariant-prf}, \texttt{covariance.js})
assigns shift degrees to rule positions mechanically.  A whole-program partial
evaluator that specializes a till program to a particular initial multiset shape
could produce a tighter accel/rebase scope, verified by a translation-validation
pass asserting state-identity of the specialized and original programs on the
shared fragment.  This is the Futamura projection repurposed for resource-aware
timed programs.

\section{Reviewer-Facing Weak Points (Honest Self-Review)}
\label{app:weak-points}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{No mechanized proofs.} Every theorem in
  \S\S\ref{sec:calculus}--\ref{sec:cohort-firing} is on-paper only.  A POPL

\item \textbf{The bridge is extra-logical and trusted.} The \texttt{settle} $\to$
  derivability direction rests on the \texttt{@fire} oracle rule being excluded
  from the cut-elimination claim and flagged \texttt{unverified: 'modeSwitch'} at
  runtime---the ``execution $=$ proof search'' slogan needs a clearer formal scope
  statement (which steps are kernel-checked, which are trusted).

\item \textbf{No formal IMTL / timed-MSR comparison.} The graded-relative
  vs.\ labeled-absolute design axis (\S\ref{sec:fenced-grades}) is asserted, not
  proven---a reviewer will ask for an embedding or separation theorem between till
  and IMTL.
\end{enumerate}
