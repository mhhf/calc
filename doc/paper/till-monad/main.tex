\title{The Delay-Graded Lax Monad:\\
  Proof Theory for Timed Multiset Rewriting}
\author{Denis Erfurt}
\date{Draft (TODO\_0270)}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Concurrent logical frameworks in the CLF tradition separate backward proof
search from forward multiset rewriting with a lax modality $\{A\}$: a proof of
$\{A\}$ records \emph{that} forward chaining can achieve $A$, but not
\emph{how long it takes}.  We present \till{}, a sequent calculus for
intuitionistic linear logic whose lax modality is \textbf{graded by a delay}
from the tropical $(\max,{+})$ dioid: availability stamps $\stmp{A}{t}$ on
hypotheses combine by $\max$ (a coeffect: synchronisation waits for the last
input), delay grades $\lax{S}{d}$ compose by $+$ (an effect: durations
accumulate along a causal chain), and a timed promotion rule stamps outputs at
$\max(\text{inputs})+d$.  Grade side conditions enter the calculus as
\emph{theory premises} --- object-level derivability goals over a decidable
arithmetic theory --- whose partial residual $\res$ makes illegal rule
instances \emph{unconstructible} rather than guarded.

We prove cut admissibility for the graded calculus --- to our knowledge the
first cut-elimination result for a graded \textbf{lax/possibility} modality;
all published graded sequent calculi with cut elimination grade the necessity
side ($\gbang{r}$), and the state of the art for lax logic proof theory is
ungraded.  We further prove identity expansion, an exact-accounting adequacy
theorem (operational stamps are the \emph{least and only} derivable production
stamps), and a \textbf{work/makespan separation theorem}: the pure graded
fragment is a \emph{cost} logic --- rules-as-hypotheses derivability computes
total sequential work ($\Sigma$ of delays) --- while the max-plus
\emph{makespan} is contributed exclusively by the stamp coeffect through the
promotion rule.  Time-as-grades is therefore a genuine extension of graded lax
logic, not a decoration.

Fused rules are uninterruptible \emph{by theorem}: in-flight work is a node of
the monadic proof term, not a state token, and interruption requires explicit
fission.  The scheduler that realises the promotion rule is deterministic and
frame-rate independent ($\settle(\settle(S,T_1),T_2) = \settle(S,T_2)$), and
serves the prover as a \emph{sound oracle} whose every step is a derivable
promotion instance.  All results are witnessed by an executable artifact: a
kernel-verified prover, an executable specification suite, and differential
fuzz harnesses over exact rational arithmetic.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{The gap}

The Concurrent Logical Framework (CLF)~\cite{Watkins2002CLF} marks the
boundary between backward proof search and committed-choice forward chaining
with a lax modality $\{A\}$, following the judgmental reconstruction of lax
logic~\cite{FairtloughMendler1997,PfenningDavies2001}.  The modality is
ungraded: a CLF derivation of $\{A\}$ certifies reachability and nothing more.

Quantitative refinements of linear logic are by now standard --- but on the
\textbf{necessity} side.  Bounded linear logic~\cite{GirardScedrovScott1992},
the coeffect calculi, quantitative type theory~\cite{Atkey2018},
Granule~\cite{OrchardLiepeltEades2019}, and a current line of graded sequent
calculi~\cite{MoonEadesOrchard2021,VollmerMarshallEadesOrchard2025,HanukaevEades2025}
all grade the exponential $\gbang{r} A$, and there cut elimination is settled.
Graded \emph{monads} are equally standard in
semantics~\cite{Katsumata2014,FujiiKatsumataMillies2016} --- as categorical
structure, with no sequent calculus.  Granule types a graded possibility
$\Diamond_r$ --- as a bidirectional type system, with no proof theory.  For the
lax modality itself, the sharpest recent result is cut elimination and uniform
interpolation for \emph{ungraded} propositional lax logic~\cite{Iemhoff2024}.
To our knowledge \textbf{no published system gives a sequent calculus with cut
elimination for a graded lax, monadic, or possibility modality} (novelty sweeps
2026-08-18 and 2026-08-21; \S\ref{sec:related}).

Meanwhile, \emph{time} in multiset rewriting is operational folklore: timed
MSR~\cite{KanovichFORMATS2016} threads a global \texttt{Time@T} fact with a
\texttt{Tick} rule; Coloured Petri Nets~\cite{Jensen2007CPN} stamp tokens and
delay outputs with exactly the operational surface we want --- and no proof
theory.  Temporal session types~\cite{DasHoffmannPfenning2018} put
discrete-time $\bigcirc$ on channel types for complexity analysis; intuitionistic
metric temporal logic (IMTL)~\cite{deSaToninhoPfenning2023} labels propositions
with absolute intervals and proves cut elimination --- with additive label
arithmetic and a constraint store, and no resource multisets.

This paper closes the square: \textbf{time enters CLF-style forward chaining as
a grade}, with the proof theory done properly.

\subsection{Contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The calculus} (\S\ref{sec:calculus}).  ILL plus stamped atoms
  $\stmp{A}{t}$ and a delay-graded lax modality $\lax{S}{d}$, in judgmental
  presentation: $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash A\tr$ and a graded lax judgment
  $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d}$.  One tropical algebra plays two roles:
  durations compose by $+$ in the graded bind; availability stamps combine by
  $\max$ in a timed promotion rule $\mathit{@fire}$ whose side condition is the
  dioid action $t \act d = t+d$.  The calculus is parametric in an
  ordered-monoid/semilattice/action triple; the tropical instance is the timed
  one.

\item \textbf{Grade side conditions as theory premises with a partial residual}
  (\S\ref{sec:calculus}).  Rules carry object-level derivability premises over a
  decidable arithmetic theory ($\thprem{!\mathit{qsub}\;F\;E\;H}$: ``$H$ is the
  residual $F \res E$'').  The residual is \emph{partial} --- no proof exists
  when $F < E$ --- so fences like ``grades never go negative'' are not side
  conditions but \emph{Grade Preservation by construction}: no rule, present or
  future, can build an invalid grade.  This is CLP-style constraint
  discharge~\cite{LassezMcAloon1990} meeting geometric-rule cut
  elimination~\cite{NegriVonPlato1998}, with output variables disciplined
  exactly as properly-oriented extra variables in deterministic
  3-CTRSs~\cite{Ohlebusch2002}.

\item \textbf{Metatheory} (\S\ref{sec:meta}).  Cut admissibility for the graded
  calculus (three cuts: linear, persistent, and a graded lax cut composing
  $d+e$), identity expansion, and completeness of the counted-exponential rules.
  Two structural observations with independent interest: the count fence $\NN$
  is precisely what makes the cut measure well-founded, while the \emph{dense}
  delay grades never enter the measure; and mismatched principal cut cases are
  \emph{vacuous} by premise partiality --- the partial residual deletes proof
  obligations rather than adding them.

\item \textbf{Adequacy, in both directions, with exact accounting}
  (\S\ref{sec:adequacy}).  \emph{Exactness:} an execution's residual is
  derivable, and a produced token's operational stamp is the least --- and only
  --- derivable production stamp (grade weakening lives on the monad, stamp
  weakening on hypotheses; production claims admit neither).  \emph{Work
  adequacy} (non-circular, no oracle in the proof path): encoding program rules
  as linear hypotheses, pure backward derivability of $\lax{\bigotimes R}{W}$
  holds iff $W$ bounds the execution's \textbf{total work} $\sum d_i$.
  \emph{Separation:} the pure graded fragment cannot express the max-plus
  \textbf{makespan} --- on a two-branch join with delays 2, 3 then 1, the least
  pure-derivable grade is 6 while the scheduler's stamp is 4.  The lax grade is
  sequential cost; parallelism is exactly what the stamp coeffect adds.

\item \textbf{Processes as proof terms; atomicity as a theorem}
  (\S\ref{sec:processes}).  A firing with delay $d$ occupies the open interval
  $(a, a{+}d)$ in which \emph{no} rule can consume or observe its effects: fused
  rules are uninterruptible by construction, and fission into \texttt{start}/\texttt{end}
  rules --- related to the fused rule by cut elimination on the fresh
  intermediate --- is the \emph{honest model} of interruptible work.  The timed
  trace is the graded monadic proof term; its settled prefix prunes to a hash
  accumulator (engineering corollary).

\item \textbf{A deterministic scheduler as a sound oracle} (\S\ref{sec:opsem},
  \S\ref{sec:adequacy}).  $\settle$ fires activation-minimal matches
  (lexicographic-first is \emph{unsound}; we give the counterexample and a
  branch-and-bound match that is sound and complete) and satisfies the
  composability law $\settle(\settle(S,T_1),T_2) = \settle(S,T_2)$ ---
  frame-rate independence for a logic-based engine.  The prover uses
  $\settle$ as an oracle rule: each step is a derivable $\mathit{@fire}$
  instance, so oracle success implies derivability (soundness); the converse
  fails and is not claimed.

\item \textbf{Artifact} (\S\ref{sec:impl}).  An implementation in the \calc{}
  proof calculus sandbox: backward prover with an independent proof-checking
  kernel (pure derivations fully verified; oracle steps disclosed as
  unverified), executable specification suite, and three differential fuzz
  harnesses (FFI $\|$ clause $\|$ bignum-reference; scheduler $\|$ oracle;
  random monad towers, kernel-checked).  Exact rational time throughout.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Non-claims}

The oracle is sound, not complete (\S\ref{sec:adequacy}); its steps are checked
structurally by the kernel and reported as unverified --- trust in a bridge
derivation reduces to trust in the scheduler, by design and disclosed.  All
proofs are on paper, none machine-checked; the mechanisation is future work.
The probabilistic grade on the weighted additive disjunction (a second axis in
the implementation) has operational semantics but no sequent rules here --- it
needs a probabilistic judgment and is a separate paper.

\section{The grade algebra: one tropical dioid, two roles}
\label{sec:algebra}
%%====================================================================

Fix the tropical dioid $\TT = (\QQ_{\geq 0}, \max, +)$.  Its fragments:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Durations (effect)} $D = (\QQ_{\geq 0}, +, 0, \leq)$, an ordered
  commutative monoid: delays compose along causal chains.
\item \textbf{Time points (coeffect)} $T = (\QQ_{\geq 0}, \max, 0, \leq)$, a
  join-semilattice: stamps synchronise at a tensor of inputs --- wait for the
  last.
\item \textbf{The action} ${\act} : T \times D \to T$, $t \act d = t+d$,
  monotone in both arguments, with $t \act 0 = t$, $(t \act d) \act e = t \act
  (d+e)$, and $\max(t,t') \act d = \max(t \act d,\; t' \act d)$ --- the last
  being precisely the semiring distributivity of $\TT$.
\end{itemize}

Every proof in this paper uses only these laws; the calculus is parametric in
any such $(D, T, \act)$ triple (\S\ref{sec:related} discusses instances).
Stamps are absolute points, delays relative translations --- the effect/coeffect
pairing is the \emph{trivial} matched pair in the sense
of~\cite{GaboardiICFP2016}; the substantive interaction is not their
distributive law $\sigma$ but the action $\act$ inside the promotion rule,
structure their framework does not supply.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Fenced grade algebras.}  Grades live inside a \emph{fence}
(validity predicate) $V$ with a \emph{partial residual}: $a \res b$ is the
$h \in V$ with $b + h = a$, undefined when none exists.  \till{}'s instances:
delays ($V = \QQ_{\geq 0}$, $\res$ defined iff $a \geq b$), counts ($V = \NN$),
weights ($V = [0,1]$, multiplicative, no residual needed --- a theorem of its
signature).  $V$ is closed under the exposed operations, which yields:

\begin{theorem}[Grade Preservation]
\label{thm:grade-pres}
If every grade literal in a program satisfies its fence and rules construct
grades only through $+$ and $\res$, every grade in every reachable state ---
forward execution and backward search alike --- satisfies its fence.  Per-rule
guards ($F \geq E$, $K \geq 1$) are derived lemmas, not stated conditions; the
force of the theorem is quantification over \emph{future} rules.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induction over firings; THY\_0022.
\end{proof}

The residual is the graded analogue of an Iris resource algebra's
validity~\cite{JungIris2018} and a residuated commutative
monoid~\cite{Galatos2007} restricted to its valid cone.  We deliberately do
\emph{not} adopt the clamping monus ($a \mathbin{\dot{-}} b = 0$ when
$b \geq a$~\cite{Amer1984}): a too-late resource must make a rule inapplicable,
not free.

\section{The calculus}
\label{sec:calculus}
%%====================================================================

\subsection{Syntax and judgments}

Formulas: ILL ($\tensor, \loli, \mathbf{1}, \addconj, \bang$) extended with
\textbf{stamped atoms} $\stmp{a}{t}$ ($t \in T$; $@$ attaches to atoms only),
\textbf{counted exponentials} $\gbang{k} A$ ($k \in \NN$), and the
\textbf{graded lax monad} $\lax{S}{d}$ ($d \in D$; bare $\{S\}$ abbreviates
$\lax{S}{0}$), $S$ ranging over the synchronous fragment as in CLF.

Judgments, with the lax judgment graded:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash A\tr$ --- $A$ holds of persistent context $\Gamma$
  and linear context $\Delta$.
\item $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d}$ --- $S$ is \emph{achievable} from
  $\Gamma; \Delta$ within delay bound $d$ (relative to the availability of the
  consumed resources; stamps make it absolute in \S\ref{subsec:stamps}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The graded lax fragment}

\begin{mathpar}
  \inferrule[\textit{lax}]
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\tr}
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{0}}
  \and
  \inferrule[\textit{sub}]
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d} \\ d \leq d'}
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d'}}
  \and
  \inferrule[\textit{\{R\}}]
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d}}
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{S}{d}\tr}
  \and
  \inferrule[\textit{\{L\}}]
    {\Gamma; \Delta, S \vdash C\laxjdg{e}}
    {\Gamma; \Delta, \lax{S}{d} \vdash C\laxjdg{d+e}}
\end{mathpar}

\noindent\textit{lax} is the unit: what is true now is achievable with zero
delay.  \textit{\{L\}} is CLF's \emph{sticky} left rule with grade composition
--- eliminating $\lax{S}{d}$ inside a lax goal adds $d$ to the bill; it fires
only under a lax conclusion, so grading rides the existing modal discipline.
\textit{sub} is subeffecting: the grade is an upper \emph{bound}, and the
operational stamp will be its least solution (\S\ref{subsec:exact}).  Left rules
of the ILL fragment are generic in the conclusion judgment.  Erasing grades
yields exactly the judgmental lax fragment of~\cite{PfenningDavies2001} ---
CLF's discipline; the calculus is a conservative decoration of it, and its proof
theory (Theorem~\ref{thm:cut}) is the graded extension of~\cite{Iemhoff2024}.

\medskip
Derived: graded $\mu$, $\lax{\lax{S}{d}}{e} \vdash \lax{S}{d+e}$;
functoriality; unit $S \vdash \lax{S}{0}$.  The $\mu$ law is the
critical-path reading of $+$: a CLF monadic let-chain accumulates the sum of
its step grades.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Counted exponentials.}  $\gbang{k} A$ is $k$ linear parcels
($A^{\tensor k}$), in the SELL/BLL tradition, with four rules (left
peel/weaken, right peel/zero) whose side conditions are arithmetic over $\NN$
($k = j{+}1$ resp.\ $k = 0$).  Completeness of the four rules for the
$A^{\tensor k}$ reading is Theorem~\ref{thm:counted-bang}.  The $\omega$
exponential $\bang A$ keeps ILL's promotion/dereliction/absorption, disjoint
from the counted grades.

\subsection{Stamps and timed promotion}
\label{subsec:stamps}

Stamps are the coeffect.  Three laws with deliberately different standings:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Retiming} (the one sequent rule; axiom): $\Gamma; \stmp{a}{t}
  \vdash \stmp{a}{t'}\tr$ for $t \leq t'$ --- delaying availability is free,
  never early.  The coeffect mirror of \textit{sub}.
\item \textbf{Ambient} $a \equiv \stmp{a}{0}$ is a canonicalisation convention at
  the state boundary, \emph{not} a rule: inside the calculus $a \not\vdash
  \stmp{a}{t}$ for every $t$ (admitting $a \vdash \stmp{a}{0}$ would compose with
  retiming into stamp forgery).  Stamps are born at the boundary, moved only by
  retiming.
\item \textbf{Monoidal} $(A \tensor B)@t \equiv \stmp{A}{t} \tensor \stmp{B}{t}$,
  $\mathbf{1}@t \equiv \mathbf{1}$ is a meta-level law of the state
  representation (compound stamped formulas are not syntax); its sequent shadow
  is derivable pointwise.
\end{itemize}

The rule tying stamps to grades is a \textbf{promotion rule} in the SELL style
--- a global condition on the context, not a local left rule:

\begin{mathpar}
  \inferrule[\textit{@fire}]
    {\Gamma; A_1, \ldots, A_n \vdash S\laxjdg{d}
     \\ a = \max(t_1, \ldots, t_n, 0)}
    {\Gamma;\; \stmp{A_1}{t_1}, \ldots, \stmp{A_n}{t_n}
     \;\vdash\; \stmp{S}{(a \act d)}\laxjdg{0}}
\end{mathpar}

\noindent Reading: the body promises $S$ within $d$ of its inputs; the inputs
synchronise at $a$ (coeffect $\max$); the outputs exist from $a+d$ (action);
the lax grade \emph{resets} --- the effect is fully internalised into stamps.
Boundary cases: $n=0$ gives $\stmp{S}{d}\laxjdg{0}$ (``the delay grade is the
stamp of the future''); $d=0$ with zero stamps is ordinary untimed firing.
Activation windows (\texttt{after} $E$ strengthens $a$; \texttt{before} $E$
bounds it) are scheduling annotations on this rule, not connectives.  Persistent
hypotheses carry no stamps; the interaction of $\omega$ with time is future
work.

In the implementation, $\mathit{@fire}$ is realised as an \emph{oracle} rule
(\S\ref{subsec:oracle}), which keeps the pure calculus's metatheory clean: cut
elimination is about the syntactic system; the oracle only ever asserts sequents
that are already derivable.

\subsection{Grade side conditions as theory premises}

The implemented calculus is single-level (one judgment; $S\laxjdg{d}$
represented as $\lax{S}{d}$ --- the equivalence is
Theorem~\ref{thm:pres-equiv}), and its rules carry \textbf{theory premises}:
object-level goals over a decidable arithmetic theory, discharged during rule
application and re-checked by the proof kernel.  The two monad rules:

\medskip
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}r@{\;\;}l@{\quad}l@{\quad}l@{}}
$\mathit{monad\_l}$: &
  $\Gamma; \Delta, \lax{A}{E} \vdash \lax{C}{F}\tr$ &
  $\Leftarrow$ &
  $\Gamma; \Delta, A \vdash \lax{C}{H}\tr\quad
   \thprem{!\mathit{qsub}\;F\;E\;H}$ \\[4pt]
$\mathit{monad\_r}$: &
  $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{A}{E}\tr$ &
  $\Leftarrow$ &
  $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash A\tr\quad
   \thprem{!\mathit{le}\;0\;E}$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\medskip

\noindent$!\mathit{qsub}\;F\;E\;H$ derives ``$H$ is the residual $F \res E$'':
it \emph{binds} the output variable $H$ when $F \geq E$ and has \textbf{no
derivation} when $F < E$ --- the rule is then simply inapplicable.  No guard
$F \geq E$ is stated anywhere; the fence is derivational
(Theorem~\ref{thm:grade-pres}).  Variables not bound by the conclusion are
output variables, bound by the derivation --- the exact discipline of properly
oriented extra variables in deterministic 3-CTRSs~\cite{Ohlebusch2002},
transported from rewriting to proof theory.

Two published lineages meet here.  The premises are CLP-style constraint goals
in the sense of the constraint sequent calculus~\cite{LassezMcAloon1990}, living
object-level in the rules.  And they are \emph{geometric}: the residual axiom
$\forall F, E.\;(F \geq E \supset \exists H.\;F = E + H)$ is a geometric
implication, so by~\cite{NegriVonPlato1998} building it into the rules preserves
cut eliminability --- in our direct proof (\S\ref{sec:meta}) this materialises
as the observation that theory premises carry no sequent-level principal formula
and therefore never obstruct a permutation.  Unlike Twelf's constraint domains
(which cannot appear in dynamic assumptions --- exactly where ours live) and
unlike deduction modulo (congruence, not functional discharge; cut elimination
conditional on super-consistency), our premises are decidable derivability goals
with functional output binding, and cut elimination is unconditional.

%%====================================================================
\section{Metatheory}
\label{sec:meta}
%%====================================================================

Full proofs: THY\_0023.  We state the results and the load-bearing points.
Throughout, ``the calculus'' is the pure syntactic system (id, the ILL rules,
$\omega$-bang, counted bangs, \textit{lax}/\textit{sub}/\textit{\{R\}}/\textit{\{L\}},
retiming, copy); the oracle rule is excluded by scoping
(\S\ref{subsec:oracle}).

\begin{theorem}[Presentation equivalence]
\label{thm:pres-equiv}
The judgmental two-level system and the implemented single-level system derive
the same sequents, with cut-free derivations mapping to cut-free derivations
both ways, size-linearly.  The glue: a $\{\}$-inversion lemma ($\lax{S}{d}\tr$
yields $S\laxjdg{d}$) and admissible grade weakening in the single-level system
--- \textit{sub} needs no primitive rule there because subeffecting lives in
$\mathit{monad\_r}$'s slack and $\mathit{monad\_l}$'s residual freedom.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Cut admissibility]
\label{thm:cut}
The three cuts
\begin{mathpar}
  \inferrule[\textit{cut}]
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash A\tr \\ \Gamma; \Delta', A \vdash J}
    {\Gamma; \Delta, \Delta' \vdash J}
  \and
  \inferrule[$\mathit{cut}_{\mathit{lax}}$]
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash S\laxjdg{d}
     \\ \Gamma; \Delta', S \vdash C\laxjdg{e}}
    {\Gamma; \Delta, \Delta' \vdash C\laxjdg{d+e}}
\end{mathpar}
\begin{mathpar}
  \inferrule[$\mathit{cut}^{!}$]
    {\Gamma; \cdot \vdash A\tr \\ \Gamma, A; \Delta \vdash J}
    {\Gamma; \Delta \vdash J}
\end{mathpar}
are admissible in the cut-free calculus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Lexicographic induction on (cut-formula weight, cut kind with
$\mathit{cut}^{!} \succ \mathit{cut}_{\mathit{lax}} \succ \mathit{cut}$, the
premise derivations).  The principal $\mathit{\{R\}}/\mathit{\{L\}}$ case
reduces a true-cut on $\lax{S}{d}$ to exactly $\mathit{cut}_{\mathit{lax}}$
with grades composing as $d+e$ --- the same composition $\mathit{\{L\}}$
performs, no new arithmetic.  $\mathit{cut}_{\mathit{lax}}$ analyses the left
derivation: the \textit{lax} case drops to \textit{cut} with the unit law
$0+e=e$; \textit{sub} commutes by monotonicity ($d_0 \leq d \Rightarrow
d_0{+}e \leq d{+}e$); $\mathit{\{L\}}$ re-associates ($d' + (d_0{+}e) =
(d'{+}d_0) + e$).  The only grade facts used are the
ordered-commutative-monoid laws --- the proof is parametric in the algebra
exactly as the calculus is.  Retiming cuts compose by transitivity of $\leq$.
Counted-bang principal cases use functionality of the residual ($J = J' = K{-}1$),
and the mismatched pairings (peel against zero) are \textbf{vacuous}: their
theory premises demand $K \geq 1$ and $K = 0$ simultaneously, so no such pair
of derivations exists --- partiality deletes case obligations.
\end{proof}

Two structural remarks worth the reader's attention:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The fence is the measure.}  Counted bangs enter the termination
  measure as $w(\gbang{K} A) = (K{+}1)(w(A){+}1)$, well-founded \emph{because}
  the count fence is $\NN$.  The delay grades --- dense in $\QQ_{\geq 0}$ ---
  never enter the measure: every monad-cut descends to the body.  Cut
  elimination is measure-blind to exactly the grade that is dense.  The fence
  (Theorem~\ref{thm:grade-pres}) is not bookkeeping; it is the induction
  principle.
\item \textbf{No erasure lift.}  THY\_0018's original sketch routed through
  grade erasure onto the ungraded lax fragment.  The direct proof does not: the
  graded system is proved outright, and the erased proof re-emerges as its
  shadow under grade deletion.
\end{itemize}

\begin{theorem}[Identity expansion]
\label{thm:id-expand}
For every formula with ground grades, $\Gamma; A \vdash A$ is derivable with
axioms only at atoms (id at unstamped atoms; retiming with reflexive $t \leq t$
at stamped atoms).  For non-ground (pattern) grades the primitive general
identity remains necessary --- symbolic-grade identity is axiomatic,
ground-grade identity admissible.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Counted-bang completeness]
\label{thm:counted-bang}
For ground $k \in \NN$, $\gbang{k} A \dashv\vdash A^{\tensor k}$ with the four
counted rules; with Theorem~\ref{thm:cut}, a sequent is provable in the counted
reading iff its expansion is provable in the bang-free fragment.  The
split/merge iso $\gbang{a+b} A \dashv\vdash \gbang{a} A \tensor \gbang{b} A$
follows.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}
\label{cor:misc}
Consistency; the analytic subformula property up to grade recomputation (every
formula in a cut-free derivation is a subformula of the end-sequent with grades
produced by $+$/$\res$ from end-sequent grades); and admissibility of the
compositions used by the adequacy and fusion theorems below --- they never leave
the cut-free calculus.
\end{corollary}

\section{Operational semantics: timed matching and settle}
\label{sec:opsem}
%%====================================================================

A \till{} program is a set of rules $\mathit{In} \loli \lax{\mathit{Out}}{d}$;
a state is a \textbf{timed multiset} --- a finite map $(\mathit{atom},
\mathit{stamp}) \to \mathit{count}$; equal-stamp copies merge into cohorts.  A
\textbf{match} $m$ assigns cohorts to pattern atoms (with counted patterns
$\gbang{k} A$ taking $k$ copies across cohorts and $\gbang{W} A$ binding a
whole cohort at firing time); its \textbf{activation} is the max-plus linear
form $a(m) = \max(\text{selected stamps} \cup \text{after-bounds})$, valid iff
below every \texttt{before}-bound.  Firing consumes at $a(m)$ and produces each
output stamped $a(m)+d$ --- one $\mathit{@fire}$ instance.

\begin{verbatim}
settle(S, T):  while some rule has a valid match m with a(m) <= T:
                 among globally minimal a(m) (ties: stateless PRF chooser);
                 fire it (consume at a(m), produce at a(m)+d)
               return S            -- quiescent at horizon T
\end{verbatim}

\noindent There is no clock, no tick, no watermark: stamps are monotone, so
$\settle$ resumes correctly after any horizon with no memory of it.

\begin{proposition}[Lexicographic-first is unsound]
\label{prop:lex-unsound}
Per-pattern oldest-first matching with backtracking does not compute the
activation-minimal match when guards couple patterns.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Counterexample]
State $\stmp{A}{0}, \stmp{A}{3}, \stmp{B}{3}, \stmp{B}{9}$, a guard rejecting exactly
$(A@0, B@3)$: lexicographic-first finds activation 9; the minimum is 3.
Firing the 9-match ahead of another rule's 5-match violates nondecreasing
activation.  A branch-and-bound search over stamp-sorted cohorts, pruning on
the partial $\max$, is sound and complete (activation is monotone in partial
assignments), and degrades to one greedy pass exactly when no guard or window
couples two patterns --- the common case costs what untimed matching costs.
THY\_0019, Props.~1--3.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Determinism and composability]
\label{thm:composability}
$\settle$ is a function of (state, horizon, seed, policies), and for
$T_1 \leq T_2$:
\[
  \settle(\settle(S,T_1),T_2) = \settle(S,T_2)
\]
--- states, traces, stamps all equal.  The proof obligation is precisely that no
selection ever reads the horizon: minimal activation is a function of the state;
equal-activation ties are resolved by a \emph{stateless} content-derived PRF
($\mathit{mix}(\mathit{seed} \oplus \mathit{hash}(\mathit{state}) \oplus
\mathit{hash}(\mathit{candidates}))$).  The law is frame-rate independence: a
game host that jumps the horizon replays exactly the history a small-stepping
host would have produced.  THY\_0019, Thms.~4--5.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Termination / Zeno guard]
\label{thm:termination}
$\settle(S,T)$ terminates iff finitely many events have activation $\leq T$;
sufficient statically: every cycle in the rule-dependency graph has positive
total delay (each traversal advances activation).  $a \loli \lax{a}{0}$ from
$\stmp{a}{0}$ is the Zeno counterexample; with $\lax{a}{1}$ the same rule is
productive --- the delay grade as a guardedness witness in the sense of
Nakano's~$\triangleright$~\cite{Nakano2000}.  Off-cycle zero-delay rules are
fine --- a strictly weaker condition than timed MSR's global progressing
condition.
\end{theorem}

\section{Adequacy}
\label{sec:adequacy}
%%====================================================================

Three results, in increasing order of what they refuse to assume.

\subsection{Exactness (operational stamps are the only production stamps)}
\label{subsec:exact}

\begin{theorem}[Exactness]
\label{thm:exact}
Let $R = \settle(\Delta, T)$ be the residual of an activation-ordered
conflict-free execution (ground grades, exact accounting).  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{(Soundness)} $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{\bigotimes R}{T}$ is
  derivable --- the execution \emph{is} a derivation: each firing is one
  $\mathit{@fire}$ instance, and composing the chain requires one
  $\mathit{\{L\}}$ residual per step, each \emph{defined} because activation
  order keeps the budget above the spent delay.  The argument appeals only to
  the residual's intrinsic partiality --- never to which other rules exist ---
  so it is stable under rule-set extension.
\item \emph{(Exactness)} for a token $\stmp{b}{u} \in R$ produced by the
  execution, the claim $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{\stmp{b}{u'}}{T'}$ is derivable
  \textbf{iff} $u' = u$: the operational stamp is the least --- and \emph{only}
  --- derivable production stamp.  A claimed stamp below the max-plus critical
  path makes some residual along the derivation undefined; a stamp above it is
  blocked because the calculus deliberately omits stamped-claim weakening.
\item \emph{(Where weakening lives)} hypothesis-side only for stamps
  ($\stmp{b}{t} \vdash \stmp{b}{t'}$ iff $t \leq t'$, retiming) and monad-side
  only for grades ($S \vdash \lax{S}{E}$ for $E \geq 0$, subeffecting).
\end{enumerate}
THY\_0018, Thm.~1.  The $\stmp{b}{u+1}$ refutation clause was found by the fuzzer
against an earlier wrong sketch that claimed production-stamp weakening.
\end{theorem}

\noindent Stamps are max-plus polynomial evaluations; the scheduler evaluates
them, the logic pins them exactly.

\begin{theorem}[Timed confluence]
\label{thm:confluence}
Conflict-free executions (no two enabled matches sharing a consumed cohort; read
arcs exempt) have order-independent residuals: the event DAG is unique, a
token's stamp is its max-plus path weight, and firing orders are
linearisations --- CLF's concurrent equality with stamps as a permutation
invariant.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Work adequacy and the separation theorem}

Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}'s soundness direction runs \emph{through} the
promotion rule.  The following is the adequacy the pure calculus supports with
no oracle at all --- and it isolates what the graded monad alone measures.

Encode a program's rules as \textbf{linear hypotheses} with multiplicities as
counted bangs: for an execution $\varepsilon$ from unstamped $\Delta_0$ with
firing multiset $n$ and residual $R$, let
$\Delta_\varepsilon = \Delta_0 \uplus
\{\gbang{n(R_i)}(In_i \loli \lax{Out_i}{d_i})\}$.

\begin{theorem}[Work adequacy]
\label{thm:work}
$\cdot;\, \Delta_\varepsilon \vdash \lax{\bigotimes R}{W}$ is derivable in the
pure calculus \textbf{iff} some execution from $\Delta_0$ with firing multiset
$n$ reaches residual $R$ and $W \geq \sum_i n(R_i)\cdot d_i$ --- the
\textbf{total sequential work}.  The $\Leftarrow$ direction replays the
execution as peel/$\loli L$/$\mathit{\{L\}}$ steps (every proof tree fully
kernel-verified in the artifact, no oracle steps); the $\Rightarrow$ direction
is the CLF adequacy permutation argument plus a telescoping residual chain:
linearity forces every rule copy to fire and every monadic output to be opened,
charging its delay exactly once.  THY\_0023, Thm.~10.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Work/makespan separation]
\label{thm:separation}
The pure grade measures work, not makespan.  Join program
$\{a \loli \lax{x}{2},\; b \loli \lax{y}{3},\; x \tensor y \loli \lax{c}{1}\}$
from $\{a, b\}$: least pure-derivable $W$ is $6 = 2{+}3{+}1$ ($\lax{c}{5}$,
$\lax{c}{4}$ refuted), while $\settle$ produces $\stmp{c}{4}$ ($\max(2,3){+}1$).
The readings coincide exactly when the firing DAG is a chain.  Moreover stamped
inputs are \emph{outside} the pure encoding entirely ($\stmp{a}{t} \not\vdash a$
--- stamps are born at the boundary).  \textbf{Consequently: the delay-graded
lax monad alone is a cost logic --- sequential composition of delays; max-plus
synchronisation is contributed exclusively by the stamp coeffect through
$\mathit{@fire}$.}  Time-as-grades is a genuine extension of graded lax logic,
and the bridge-soundness theorem below is exactly the statement that the
extension is consistent.  THY\_0023, Thm.~11; both columns pinned by test.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{The oracle rule (bridge soundness)}
\label{subsec:oracle}

The implementation realises $\mathit{@fire}$ as an admissible \textbf{oracle
rule}: for a goal $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{S}{T}$ the prover may run
$\settle(\Delta, T)$ --- reading the succedent grade as the observation horizon
--- and then prove $S$ against the residual timed multiset by the pure calculus
(retiming + decomposition + identity).

\begin{theorem}[Oracle soundness]
\label{thm:oracle}
If the oracle succeeds, $\Gamma; \Delta \vdash \lax{S}{T}$ is derivable: each
settle step is an $\mathit{@fire}$ instance
(Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}'s compositionality), and the residual match is an
ordinary derivation.  The \textbf{converse is false and not claimed}:
\textit{sub} derives $a \vdash \lax{a}{d}$ for every $d \geq 0$ with no forward
step, so derivability does not imply $\settle$-reachability.  The implemented
prover searches both the pure calculus and the oracle, so \emph{prover} failure
refutes the sequent; oracle failure alone refutes only the oracle route.
\end{theorem}

Kernel checking of an oracle step is structural: the settle run is trusted and
\emph{disclosed} --- the checker reports it as an unverified step, and ``fully
verified'' is reserved for pure derivations.  All Stage-1 results (\S\ref{sec:meta})
and all of Theorem~\ref{thm:work}'s derivations meet full verification;
Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}'s bridge derivations are verified modulo the disclosed
oracle step, by design.

\section{Processes as proof terms: Fusion = Atomicity}
\label{sec:processes}
%%====================================================================

\begin{theorem}[Fusion = Atomicity]
\label{thm:fusion}
\begin{enumerate}[(a)]
\item \emph{Atomicity of fused rules.}  For a firing at activation $a$ with
  delay $d$, inputs are consumed at $a$ and every output carries stamp $a+d$;
  any match involving an output --- consuming \emph{or reading}, since read
  stamps join the activation max --- has activation $\geq a+d$.  The open
  interval $(a, a{+}d)$ admits no interaction with the job: fused rules are
  uninterruptible by construction.
\item \emph{Fission.}  Replacing
  $\mathit{fused} = \mathit{In} \loli \lax{\mathit{Out}}{d}$ by
  $\mathit{start} = \mathit{In} \loli \lax{J}{0}$,
  $\mathit{end} = J \loli \lax{\mathit{Out}}{d}$
  ($J$ fresh) is observationally equivalent on $J$-free observables with
  identical stamps: $\mathit{start};\mathit{end}$ composes by graded $\mu$
  ($0+d = d$), and fusion is cut elimination on $J$ --- the grade $@d$ is what
  remains of the cut.
\item \emph{Corollary.}  An interruptible process \textbf{must} be fissioned:
  the mid-flight resource (\texttt{constructing(house)}) is the honest model of
  a job the world can touch.  Observability never requires fission --- the job
  is visible as a node of the trace; only \emph{interaction} does.
\end{enumerate}
THY\_0018, Thms.~3+5, merged; the isolation argument of (a) is the proof
technique for the headline law (b).
\end{theorem}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Read arcs.}  $\mathtt{read}\;A$ joins the activation max,
consumes nothing, and re-emits the token bit-identically with its original
stamp; in conflict-free activation-ordered executions original-stamp and
activation-stamp conventions are observationally equivalent (a scheduler
property transferring to the logic through Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}), and the
original-stamp choice makes re-emission a state no-op.  Concurrent same-instant
reads do not conflict --- a consume-and-reproduce encoding would falsely
serialise them.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Trace $\cong$ term (observation, with an engineering
corollary).}  The flat step log (rule, $\theta$, $a$, $d$ in firing order) and
the CLF monadic proof term (the let-chain) are two presentations of one object;
the stamps make the concurrency partial order explicit, and the log is its
canonical policy-fixed linearisation.  This identification is CLF's design;
what is new is only the per-node stamp and the canonicality inherited from
Theorem~\ref{thm:composability}.

\begin{remark}[Engineering corollary]
Reassociating the term as an event snoc-list lets a running system prune the
settled past into a hash accumulator $H_k = \mathit{hash}(H_{k-1}, \mathit{ev}_k)$
--- $O(1)$ history storage that still cryptographically commits to the full
trace; what pruning preserves is exactly the commitment, nothing else.
\end{remark}

\section{Implementation and evaluation}
\label{sec:impl}
%%====================================================================

\subsection{The artifact}

\till{} is implemented in \calc{}, a proof-calculus sandbox with a
content-addressed formula store (formulas are hashes; $O(1)$ equality).
Components: the backward focused prover over the declarative rule file (template
rules with theory premises; the arithmetic theory is itself a logic program over
exact rationals, with an FFI decision-procedure face as an optimisation ---
\emph{theory is semantics, FFI is optimisation}: every FFI predicate has clause
definitions, and a no-FFI mode runs the entire test surface on the clause face
alone); the independent proof-checking kernel (re-derives every step including
theory premises, re-threads linear resource accounting, rejects
context-leaking forgeries; oracle and eigenvariable steps disclosed in an
\texttt{unverified} report); the timed scheduler (branch-and-bound
minimal-activation matching, stateless PRF chooser); and a TTY shell for live
timed programs.

\subsection{Executable metatheory}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Provability grid}: the \S\ref{sec:meta} fragment as $\sim$90
  pinned provable/refuted sequents, every proof kernel-verified with zero
  unverified steps.
\item \textbf{Adequacy suites}: executable specifications whose
  \texttt{\#expect} gates are wrapped as sequents
  (Theorem~\ref{thm:exact} soundness + refutations;
  Theorem~\ref{thm:fusion} as (under)derivability); the pure work-adequacy
  suite (Theorems~\ref{thm:work}/\ref{thm:separation}, no oracle in any proof
  path, oracle column cross-checked against a from-the-spec reference
  implementation).
\item \textbf{Differential fuzzing}: (1) grade arithmetic three ways --- FFI
  $\|$ clause resolution $\|$ BigInt reference --- including residual
  definedness; (2) scheduler vs the executable reference semantics; (3) random
  monad towers $\lax{\cdots\lax{a}{d_1}\cdots}{d_n} \vdash \lax{a}{f}$ against
  the $\sum d$ oracle, plus retiming and production-claim exactness on random
  one-rule programs --- every success kernel-verified.  The exactness clause of
  Theorem~\ref{thm:exact} was \emph{corrected by this harness} (an earlier
  sketch wrongly admitted production-stamp weakening; the fuzzer found the
  counterexample) --- we consider this the strongest argument for executable
  metatheory as a paper artifact.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Case studies and benchmarks}

Two case studies exercise the full surface: a delay-scheduled economy
(activation windows, spoilage races, counted parcels) and a weighted duel (the
probabilistic axis, out of scope here).  The internal baseline and the
measurement protocol (state sizes, horizon sweeps, match-search stress with
coupled guards) are fixed in the artifact; the cross-system table against
Real-Time Maude, CPN Tools, and Ceptre requires those tools' hosts and is
\textbf{pending} --- reviewers should read \S\ref{sec:impl} as
artifact-functionality evidence, not comparative performance claims, until that
table lands.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%%====================================================================

Grading CLF's lax monad by a tropical delay yields a sequent calculus where
scheduling \emph{is} proof search structure: durations compose in the graded
bind, synchronisation is a promotion side condition, in-flight work is a
proof-term node, and the scheduler is a sound, deterministic,
frame-rate-independent oracle.  The metatheory is done on paper in full --- cut
admissibility, identity expansion, exact adequacy, and a separation theorem
showing the grade alone measures cost while stamps alone contribute scheduling.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Future work.}  Mechanisation of \S\ref{sec:meta} (the rules
must carry the theory-premise form).  The probabilistic weight grade (weighted
additive disjunction) needs its own judgment --- a second paper.  Symbolic timed
exploration (metavariables in both pattern and state) and the $\omega$-exponential
$\times$ time interaction remain open.  The cross-system benchmark table is
pending a controlled host.
